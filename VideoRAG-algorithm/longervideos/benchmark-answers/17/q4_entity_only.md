Question: How does Decision Lens software address the challenge of planning in silos?

### How Decision Lens Software Addresses the Challenge of Planning in Silos

Decision Lens software tackles the issue of planning in silos by integrating planning processes across different levels and functions within organizations. This is achieved through several key features:

1. **Integrated Planning Across Time Horizons**:
   - Decision Lens allows planners to assess the current environment and establish priorities that span multiple time horizons, ensuring a cohesive view over extended periods. Instead of viewing planning as a one-time event, it facilitates continuous planning and evaluation.

2. **Connecting Functions and Portfolios**:
   - The software connects functions to bring together multiple portfolios and metrics, breaking down silos by enabling cross-functional collaboration. This integration ensures that plans are not isolated but are linked and coordinated across different departments and divisions.

3. **Balancing Sustenance and Transformation**:
   - By balancing sustenance and transformation, Decision Lens helps organizations align short-term operational needs with long-term strategic goals. This holistic approach prevents siloed thinking by promoting a unified vision across the organization.

4. **Hierarchical Management**:
   - Decision Lens supports hierarchical management, allowing planners to establish priorities at various levels—from the lowest operational tasks to the highest strategic objectives. This ensures that all levels of the organization are aligned and supportive of overarching goals.

5. **Scenario Planning and Resource Optimization**:
   - The software enables scenario planning, allowing organizations to evaluate multiple alternatives and optimize resource allocation. This feature helps in making informed decisions that consider the broader impact on the organization, reducing the likelihood of isolated planning efforts.

6. **Application of Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP)**:
   - Decision Lens utilizes the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to establish priorities and evaluate options systematically. This method helps in making value-based decisions by comparing different factors and alternatives, ensuring that decisions are well-rounded and not confined to individual silos.

7. **Real-Time Evaluation and Continuous Improvement**:
   - Decision Lens supports real-time evaluation and continuous improvement, enabling organizations to adapt plans dynamically based on changing circumstances. This flexibility helps in overcoming rigid siloed approaches by fostering an environment of ongoing assessment and adjustment.

By leveraging these features, Decision Lens software helps organizations overcome the challenges posed by siloed planning, ensuring that plans are integrated, aligned, and optimized for overall organizational success.
