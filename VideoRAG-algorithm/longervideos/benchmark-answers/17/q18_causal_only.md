The Navy has adjusted its training schedules and logistical planning to ensure that naval forces are better prepared to respond to crises, including those that might arise from geopolitical tensions elsewhere.

1. **Budget Reallocation**: Resources previously allocated to less urgent projects may be redirected to support immediate needs.
