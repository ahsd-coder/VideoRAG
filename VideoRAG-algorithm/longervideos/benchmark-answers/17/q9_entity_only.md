### Decision Lens Software and Its Impact on UNOS Organ Allocation Decisions

#### Overview of Decision Lens Software
Decision Lens software is designed to enhance strategic planning and decision-making processes by connecting across time horizons, functions, and organizational hierarchies. This modernized approach helps organizations like UNOS to optimize their investment strategies and balance sustainability with transformation, ensuring effective support in both current and future scenarios.

#### Application in UNOS Context
The United Network for Organ Sharing (UNOS) utilizes Decision Lens to improve its organ allocation processes, aiming to reduce waitlist deaths and ensure equitable access to organs. The software integrates various medical criteria, such as medical urgency, pediatric priority, and travel efficiency, to streamline decision-making and achieve better outcomes.

#### Key Features and Benefits
1. **Connecting Across Time Horizons**:
   - Decision Lens enables planners to link planning horizons effectively, allowing for a dynamic and adaptable approach to organ allocation. This ensures that decisions are not based on a single snapshot but rather on a comprehensive timeline, accounting for evolving patient needs and medical advancements.

2. **Connecting Across Functions**:
   - By integrating multiple portfolios and establishing dependencies, Decision Lens facilitates a cohesive approach to organ allocation. This means that different departments and stakeholders, such as medical teams, logistics, and administrative staff, can collaborate seamlessly, ensuring that all aspects of organ distribution are considered.

3. **Connecting Up and Down the Organizational Hierarchy**:
   - Balancing sustainability with transformation, Decision Lens supports effective decision-making at various levels within UNOS. This ensures that both immediate and long-term goals are aligned, from grassroots efforts to high-level strategic decisions.

#### Improvements in Organ Allocation
1. **Reduction in Waitlist Deaths**:
   - According to presentations by Decision Lens, the implementation of their software has led to a significant decline in waitlist deaths. For instance, under continuous allocation scenarios, the number of deaths decreased from 438 under current rules to 208 in a 2:1 Living Donor Allocation System (LAS).

2. **Incorporation of Multiple Criteria**:
   - Decision Lens incorporates various factors in organ allocation, including medical urgency, pediatric priority, and travel efficiency. These criteria ensure that organs are allocated based on a holistic assessment of patient needs, rather than isolated factors.

3. **Equitable Treatment of Candidates**:
   - The software emphasizes equitable treatment, ensuring that candidates who have previously donated organs receive priority. Additionally, it considers the physical health background of different tiers of the population, promoting fairness in organ distribution.

4. **Avoidance of Feudal Transplants**:
   - Decision Lens helps UNOS avoid quick allocations to individuals with high metaphorical needs but low likelihood of success. For example, a person with a lifelong alcohol addiction requiring a liver transplant would be evaluated carefully to prevent wastage of scarce resources.

#### Conclusion
Through the use of Decision Lens software, UNOS has made significant strides in improving the efficiency and equity of organ allocation decisions. By leveraging advanced planning tools, UNOS can now consider a broader range of factors and adapt to changing circumstances, ultimately leading to better patient outcomes and more effective use of available organs.
