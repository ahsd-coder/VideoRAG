### Addressing Planning Silos with Decision Lens Software

**Introduction**
Planning in silos is a common challenge within organizations, where different departments or teams work independently, leading to fragmented strategies and inefficiencies. Decision Lens software offers a comprehensive solution to overcome this issue by fostering integrated planning and decision-making processes.

**Key Features and Benefits**

1. **Integrated Planning Capabilities**:
   - **Connecting Across Time Horizons**: Decision Lens enables planners to connect across various time horizons, ensuring that short-term and long-term goals are aligned. This feature helps break down the siloed approach by providing a continuous view of the planning process, akin to a "movie" rather than a static snapshot.
   
2. **Linking Across Functions**:
   - **Interdepartmental Coordination**: The software facilitates linking across different functions within an organization, bringing together multiple portfolios and establishing dependencies. This ensures that planning efforts are not isolated but are interconnected, promoting a holistic approach to decision-making.
   
3. **Balancing Organizational Hierarchies**:
   - **Top-to-Bottom Integration**: Decision Lens supports balancing between sustaining current operations and transforming for future readiness. By integrating the needs across various asset areas and managing organizational hierarchies, it ensures that decisions at the lowest levels support the highest strategic objectives.
   
4. **Scenario Planning and Continuous Evaluation**:
   - **Dynamic Planning Environment**: Unlike traditional episodic planning models, Decision Lens promotes a continuous and dynamic planning environment. Organizations can continually make trade-offs and reframe questions based on evolving circumstances, adapting to changes more effectively.

**Example Use Cases**

1. **Government Sector**:
   - **United Network for Organ Sharing**: Decision Lens has been instrumental in improving organ allocation scenarios, reducing waitlist deaths by optimizing resource distribution and prioritizing medical urgency, travel efficiency, and other critical factors.
   
2. **Military Applications**:
   - **Navy Aircraft Carrier Groups**: The software integrates planning processes for different components of an aircraft carrier group, ensuring that all aspects—from aircraft to ship operations—are coordinated seamlessly.
   
3. **Commercial Enterprises**:
   - **T-Mobile**: Decision Lens has been adopted by commercial organizations like T-Mobile to enhance decision-making processes, leveraging the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) for establishing value and optimizing resource allocation.

**Conclusion**
Decision Lens software addresses the challenge of planning in silos by offering robust integrated planning capabilities that link across time horizons, functions, and organizational hierarchies. Its continuous and dynamic approach ensures that planning processes are cohesive, adaptable, and aligned with overarching strategic objectives. Through its application in diverse sectors, Decision Lens demonstrates its effectiveness in fostering a unified and efficient decision-making environment.
