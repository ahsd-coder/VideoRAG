### Criteria Used by the National Park Service in Prioritizing Environmental Cleanup Projects

The National Park Service (NPS) employs a structured approach to prioritize environmental cleanup projects through its Environmental Compliance and Cleanup Division (ECCD). This division utilizes a hierarchical model of criteria designed to ensure comprehensive and strategic decision-making. Here’s a summary of the key criteria:

#### Hierarchical Model Overview
The hierarchical model consists of several layers of goals, subcriteria, and source policies. Goals represent overarching objectives, while subcriteria provide more specific guidance on how to achieve these goals. Source policies outline the foundational principles and regulatory frameworks that support each criterion.

#### Goals and Subcriteria
The primary goals and associated subcriteria are outlined below:

1. **Protect Human Health and Safety**
   - Reduce hazardous contaminants
   - Enhance public awareness and education
   - Implement risk management strategies

2. **Enhance Access to Public Lands**
   - Increase reuse and recycling
   - Improve recreational facilities and infrastructure
   - Ensure accessibility and inclusivity

3. **Stimulate Economic Activity**
   - Leverage private investments
   - Create job opportunities through remediation efforts
   - Promote sustainable tourism

4. **Address Legacy Impacts**
   - Remediate historical contamination
   - Restore ecosystems and habitats
   - Mitigate long-term environmental damage

5. **Reduce Liability Issues**
   - Establish clear accountability and responsibility
   - Streamline regulatory compliance
   - Manage legal and financial risks

6. **Ensure Responsible Stewardship**
   - Collaborate with local communities and stakeholders
   - Foster partnerships with federal, state, and private entities
   - Promote transparency and public trust

#### Evaluation Framework
To effectively prioritize projects, the NPS uses a rigorous evaluation framework that assesses each criterion based on predefined metrics. For instance:

- **Project Impact**: Evaluates the potential benefits and drawbacks of a project in terms of environmental, economic, and social impacts.
- **Feasibility**: Considers the practicality of implementing a project, including available resources, technical expertise, and logistical constraints.
- **Risk Management**: Assesses potential risks and identifies mitigation strategies to minimize adverse effects.

#### Decision-Making Process
The decision-making process involves multiple stakeholders and incorporates various perspectives. This includes:

- **Technical Committees**: Specialized groups that evaluate projects based on technical merit and feasibility.
- **Business Benefit Committees**: Teams that assess the broader economic and social benefits of projects.
- **Public Engagement**: Opportunities for community input and feedback to ensure projects align with public interests and values.

#### Software Tools
To facilitate this complex decision-making process, the NPS leverages advanced software tools like Decision Lens. These tools enable the integration of diverse data sources and support systematic analysis of criteria, helping to identify optimal project priorities.

### 国家公园管理局优先考虑环境清理项目的标准

国家公园管理局（NPS）通过其环境合规和清理部门（ECCD）采用了一种结构化的决策方法来优先考虑环境清理项目。该部门使用一个分层模型的标准，以确保全面和战略性决策。以下是对这些标准的概述：

#### 分层模型概览
分层模型包括多个层次的目标、子标准和源政策。目标代表总体目标，而子标准则提供了实现这些目标的具体指导。源政策则说明了每个标准背后的基本原则和法规框架。

#### 目标与子标准
主要目标及其相关子标准如下：

1. **保护人类健康与安全**
   - 减少有害污染物
   - 提高公众意识和教育
   - 实施风险管理策略

2. **增强公共土地的可访问性**
   - 增加回收利用
   - 改善娱乐设施和基础设施
   - 确保可达性和包容性

3. **刺激经济活动**
   - 利用私人投资
   - 通过整治工作创造就业机会
   - 推动可持续旅游

4. **解决历史遗留问题**
   - 治理历史污染
   - 恢复生态系统和栖息地
   - 缓解长期环境损害

5. **减少法律责任问题**
   - 明确责任和义务
   - 简化监管合规
   - 管理法律和财务风险

6. **确保负责任的管理**
   - 与当地社区和利益相关者合作
   - 培养联邦、州和私营实体之间的伙伴关系
   - 推动透明度和公众信任

#### 评估框架
为了有效优先处理项目，NPS使用严格的评估框架来评估每个标准基于预定义的指标。例如：

- **项目影响**：评估项目的潜在好处和弊端，包括环境、经济和社会影响。
- **可行性**：考虑实施项目的实际性，包括可用资源、技术专长和后勤约束条件。
- **风险管理**：评估潜在风险，并确定缓解策略以最小化不利影响。

#### 决策过程
决策过程涉及多个利益相关方，并结合各种视角。这包括：

- **技术委员会**：专门小组根据技术优势和可行性评估项目。
- **商业效益委员会**：团队评估项目的更广泛经济和社会效益。
- **公众参与**：提供社区意见和反馈的机会，确保项目符合公众利益和价值观。

#### 软件工具
为支持复杂的决策过程，NPS利用了高级软件工具，如Decision Lens。这些工具能够整合各种数据来源，并支持系统分析标准，帮助识别最佳项目优先级。
