### Global Figures on Children's Education

Several specific global figures on children's education have been highlighted in the retrieved information:

1. **Girls Out of School**:
   - **130 million girls**: According to one video, approximately 130 million girls are currently out of school, with more than two-thirds of them being of secondary school age.
   - **Barriers to Learning**: The video also mentions that conflict, poverty, gender-based violence, inadequate sanitary provisions, and early child marriage are significant obstacles preventing these girls from learning.

2. **Refugee Children's Education**:
   - **Primary Education**: Only two-thirds of refugee children have access to primary education.
   - **Secondary Education**: Only one-third of refugee children have access to secondary education.
   - **University Access**: Only 1% of refugee children have access to university education.

3. **Children Globally Not in School**:
   - **260 Million Children**: More than 260 million children are out of school worldwide.
   - **Progress and Current Situation**: Despite a 40% decrease since the year 2000, 244 million children will still not be going to school this year.

4. **Literacy Rates Among 10-Year-Olds**:
   - **57% to 70% Decline**: Before the COVID-19 pandemic, 57% of 10-year-olds could not understand a simple written text. This figure has risen to 70%, with sub-Saharan Africa experiencing an even higher rate of 89%.

5. **Education Funding Shortfall**:
   - **Underfunding**: The global community is spending less than half of the amount needed on education.

These figures underscore significant challenges in achieving equitable and inclusive education for children, particularly girls and refugees, highlighting the urgent need for increased investment and commitment from the international community.
