### How Education Cannot Wait's Model Facilitates Its Reach

Education Cannot Wait (ECW) aims to provide education for children and adolescents affected by crises. The model facilitating ECW’s reach involves several key components:

#### Collaborative Approach
ECW operates through a collaborative framework that brings together multiple actors, including governments, NGOs, and international organizations. This is evident in the following statements:

- **Collaboration Among Stakeholders**: The model relies on joint programming and financial investments from various actors, as mentioned by speakers in the retrieved transcripts. For instance, one speaker highlighted that ECW works with partners such as the High Commissioner for Refugees, UNICEF, World Food Program, UNESCO, Save the Children, and numerous local NGOs and civil society groups.

#### Focus on Co-Creation and Co-Design
The initiative emphasizes co-creation and co-design phases for new projects, ensuring that local needs are met effectively. This is reflected in the following excerpt:

- **Co-Creation Phases**: Speakers noted that ECW spends considerable time in the co-creation and co-design phases, which involve working closely with local communities and organizations to tailor initiatives to specific contexts.

#### Bridging Development and Humanitarian Work
ECW aims to bridge the gap between development and humanitarian work, ensuring that education is not overlooked in times of crisis. This is illustrated by the following points:

- **Bridging Divides**: A speaker emphasized the importance of addressing the issue of education slipping through the cracks between development and humanitarian work. ECW seeks to integrate these efforts to ensure continuity in educational programs, even in conflict-affected regions.

#### Joint Programming and Financial Investments
Joint programming and financial investments are core to ECW’s operational model, ensuring that resources are efficiently utilized to achieve educational goals. Examples include:

- **Financial Commitments**: ECW has secured significant financial commitments from member states and international partners. For instance, a speaker mentioned that ECW has received an additional $1 billion in alignment with its programs, totaling over $2 billion towards joint programming in education.

#### Emphasis on Results and Accountability
ECW places a strong emphasis on delivering results and maintaining accountability to ensure that resources are effectively utilized. This is highlighted by the following points:

- **Focus on Results**: Speakers stressed the importance of focusing on results and delivering on commitments. This includes leveraging social media platforms to spread awareness and maintain public pressure for continued support.

#### Addressing Barriers and Challenges
ECW acknowledges and addresses the barriers that limit young people’s participation in educational initiatives, such as BISA (Board of International Students Associations). This is shown in the following statement:

- **Addressing Barriers**: A speaker acknowledged the existence of barriers like BISA that limit young people’s participation and emphasized the need to address these issues moving forward.

By integrating these components, ECW aims to reach and support children and adolescents affected by crises, ensuring that education remains a priority even in challenging circumstances.
