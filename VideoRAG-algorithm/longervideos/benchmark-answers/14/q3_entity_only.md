### Funding Sources for 'Education Cannot Wait'

**Introduction**
Education Cannot Wait (ECW) is a global fund dedicated to ensuring education for children and youth affected by crises. The funding for ECW comes from various sources, including governments, international organizations, and private sector entities.

**Government Contributions**
Several governments have been significant contributors to ECW:

- **Germany**: Donated nearly €20 million in a single year.
- **United Kingdom**: Provided substantial funding, though the exact amount is not specified in the available data.
- **United States**: Contributed significantly to ECW.
- **Denmark**: Made notable donations to ECW.
- **Norway**: Also contributed to ECW.
- **Canada**: Supported ECW financially.
- **European Commission**: Contributed to ECW as well.

These contributions highlight the commitment of multiple countries to supporting education in crisis-affected regions.

**Private Sector Contributions**
Private sector entities have also played a crucial role in funding ECW:

- **Lego Foundation**: Has been recognized for its significant contributions, bringing both financial investment and creative educational approaches to children and youth in conflict zones.

**International Organizations**
Various international organizations have partnered with ECW to provide financial and logistical support:

- **UNESCO**: Collaborates with ECW to advocate for increased funding and policy changes that support education in crises.
- **United Nations Agencies**: Including UNICEF, WFP, and UNHCR, have provided financial and operational support to ECW's initiatives.

**Other Funding Mechanisms**
Additional funding mechanisms have been utilized to support ECW's mission:

- **International Finance Facility for Education (IFFEd)**: Mobilizes funds to support education in lower-middle-income countries.
- **Joint Programming Initiatives**: ECW has secured over $2 billion towards joint programming in education, leveraging additional funding from member states and international missions.

**Conclusion**
Education Cannot Wait receives funding from a diverse range of sources, including governments, private sector entities, and international organizations. This multi-faceted approach ensures that ECW can effectively address the educational needs of children and youth affected by crises worldwide.
