### How Education Cannot Wait's Model Facilitates Its Reach

**Overview:**
Education Cannot Wait (ECW) is a global fund dedicated to providing quality education for children and adolescents affected by crises. The model facilitates its reach through a combination of strategic partnerships, innovative initiatives, and a focus on marginalized communities.

#### Strategic Partnerships and Collaborations

- **Co-Creation and Co-Design:** ECW emphasizes collaborative efforts in designing and implementing its initiatives. This involves working closely with various stakeholders, including governments, NGOs, and community leaders, to ensure that educational programs are tailored to the specific needs of crisis-affected regions (T30-T33).

- **Special Envoy and Board Chair Involvement:** The fund's initiatives are spearheaded by a Special Envoy, working alongside the Board Chair, Julia Gillard, to kick off new projects and ensure alignment with broader educational goals (T31).

#### Innovative Initiatives and Funding

- **Co-Creation Phase:** ECW dedicates substantial time to the co-creation and co-design phases of its initiatives, ensuring that programs are developed with input from local communities and experts. This approach enhances the relevance and effectiveness of the educational interventions (T32).

- **Financial Mobilization:** ECW has successfully mobilized resources, securing significant funding from member states and other sources. Since becoming operational in 2017, ECW has reached nearly 7 million children and youth, demonstrating its capacity to scale up initiatives rapidly (T3).

#### Focus on Marginalized Communities

- **Inclusive Dialogue and Broad Engagement:** ECW prioritizes inclusive dialogue to address barriers that prevent marginalized groups from accessing education. By fostering broad engagement, ECW ensures that its initiatives are accessible to all, regardless of their background (T57).

- **Support for Vulnerable Populations:** ECW works extensively with refugee communities and other marginalized groups, providing essential educational support and resources to bridge the gap in access to education (T58).

#### Operational Structure and Coordination

- **UN Affiliation:** ECW leverages its affiliation with the United Nations to reach those left furthest behind. This affiliation allows ECW to operate within a robust framework that supports its mission and ensures accountability (T4).

- **Proven Models and Evaluations:** ECW utilizes proven models that have been validated through independent evaluations. These models are designed to maximize impact and ensure that educational programs are effective and sustainable (T6).

#### Advocacy and Awareness

- **Public Statements and Media Coverage:** ECW leaders and representatives frequently participate in press conferences and public statements to raise awareness about the importance of education in crisis contexts. These engagements help to garner support and resources from a wide range of stakeholders (T45).

By combining these strategies, Education Cannot Wait is able to reach and support millions of children and adolescents in crisis-affected regions, ensuring that they have access to quality education despite challenging circumstances.
