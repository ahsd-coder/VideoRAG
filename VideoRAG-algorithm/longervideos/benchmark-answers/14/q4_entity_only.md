Question: What are the goals of the Transforming Education Summit?

### Goals of the Transforming Education Summit

The Transforming Education Summit (TES) is a high-level event aimed at addressing the global crisis in education, which includes issues of access, quality, and relevance. Here are some of the key goals of the summit:

#### 1. **Addressing the Global Crisis in Education**
   - **Access:** Ensure that every individual has access to quality education.
   - **Quality:** Improve the quality of education to meet the needs of the 21st century.
   - **Relevance:** Make education relevant to the current and future challenges faced by societies.

#### 2. **Promoting Digital Technologies**
   - Encourage the promotion of digital technologies, including access to broadband internet and technology devices, connectivity, digital inclusion, and literacy.
   - Integrate digital competences into educational systems to prepare learners for the digital age.

#### 3. **Enhancing Youth Participation**
   - Increase youth involvement in education transformation processes.
   - Empower young people to participate in decision-making and governance structures related to education.

#### 4. **Improving Educational Policies and Practices**
   - Work towards the transformation of education systems to achieve Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG4).
   - Translate commitments into actions and policies to fulfill the vision of Agenda 2030 and SDG4.

#### 5. **Collaboration and Partnership**
   - Foster collaboration between Member States, youth, civil society, and other key education stakeholders.
   - Leverage collective efforts to address systemic challenges in education.

#### 6. **Sustainable Education Systems**
   - Protect schools as unique and irreplaceable institutions while transforming them to become more open, diverse, and collaborative.
   - Create new educational ecosystems that favor cooperation, curricula based on the knowledge commons, and a more collaborative teaching profession.

#### 7. **Financing and Resource Allocation**
   - Allocate adequate financial resources to education.
   - Increase aid and set fair global tax systems to enable low-income countries to spend more on education.

#### 8. **Global Commitment and Accountability**
   - Ensure that commitments made during the summit are realized and implemented.
   - Hold stakeholders accountable for their promises and actions.

### Context and Setting

The summit takes place in a formal and professional setting, often within the United Nations General Assembly Hall or other significant venues. The atmosphere is characterized by high-level discussions, speeches, and panel sessions featuring dignitaries, ministers, youth leaders, and representatives from international organizations.

### Conclusion

The Transforming Education Summit seeks to catalyze global action towards achieving quality education for all, leveraging the power of technology, youth engagement, and international collaboration. By addressing the multifaceted challenges in education, the summit aims to create a transformative impact on educational systems worldwide.
