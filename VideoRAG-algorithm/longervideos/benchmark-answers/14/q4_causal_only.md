Question: What are the goals of the Transforming Education Summit?

### Goals of the Transforming Education Summit

The Transforming Education Summit aims to address and resolve significant global issues in education. Here are some of the key goals outlined from the retrieved information:

#### 1. **Highlighting the Importance of Education**
   - **Emphasis on Education**: The summit underscores the critical role of education in transforming lives, economies, and societies. Education is described as a fundamental goal for humanity's progress.
   - **Addressing the Crisis**: It recognizes that education is currently in a deep crisis, necessitating transformative changes to ensure it becomes a positive force rather than a hindrance.

#### 2. **Promoting Universal Access and Quality Education**
   - **Accessibility**: The summit aims to ensure that education is accessible to all, addressing issues of access, quality, and relevance.
   - **Quality Education**: Emphasis is placed on providing quality education that supports individual learners and equips them with essential skills for the 21st century, such as problem-solving and collaboration.

#### 3. **Involving Stakeholders in Decision-Making**
   - **Cross-Cultural Priorities**: The summit focuses on cross-cutting priorities for transforming education, involving various stakeholders including youth, science, faith-based organizations, and civil society.
   - **Youth Participation**: Engaging young people in decision-making processes is a key goal, recognizing their role in shaping the future and advocating for transformative changes.

#### 4. **Fostering International Collaboration and Financing**
   - **International Cooperation**: The summit seeks to foster international cooperation and collaboration among member states, youth, and civil society to achieve Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG4).
   - **Financing**: Ensuring adequate financing for education is a priority, with discussions on increasing aid, canceling debts, and establishing a fair global tax system to support education, especially for girls.

#### 5. **Implementing Transformative Changes**
   - **Commitments and Actions**: The summit aims to translate commitments into actionable policies and programs, ensuring that promises made are fulfilled.
   - **Digital Technologies**: Promoting digital technologies, including access to broadband internet and technology devices, connectivity, digital inclusion, and literacy, is a key focus.
   - **Curriculum Reforms**: Integrating digital competencies into educational systems and upgrading curricula to meet the needs of the 21st century.

#### 6. **Empowering Young People**
   - **Youth Leadership**: Encouraging youth to lead the way in transforming education, leveraging their voices and ideas to drive change.
   - **Social Contracts**: Rethinking future social contracts for education to ensure it is recognized as a common good and a global responsibility.

### Summary
The Transforming Education Summit aims to address the global crisis in education by promoting universal access to quality education, involving all stakeholders in decision-making, fostering international collaboration, implementing transformative changes, and empowering young people to lead the way. These goals reflect a comprehensive approach to ensuring that education fulfills its potential to transform lives and societies.
