### Bridging the Gap Between Humanitarian Aid and Development Assistance Through Education Cannot Wait

**Introduction**
Education Cannot Wait (ECW) is a global fund that aims to bridge the gap between humanitarian aid and development assistance in crisis-affected contexts. The organization works to ensure that children and adolescents have access to quality education during times of conflict, natural disasters, and other emergencies. Here's how ECW achieves this:

#### Collaboration and Partnerships
ECW collaborates with multiple international organizations, including the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), UNICEF, World Food Programme (WFP), UNESCO, Save the Children, Jesuit Refugee Service, Plan International, Norwegian Refugee Council, and many local NGOs and civil society groups. These partnerships enable ECW to mobilize resources, expertise, and influence to address educational needs effectively.

#### Co-Creation and Co-Design
ECW emphasizes co-creation and co-design phases in its initiatives, ensuring that education programs are tailored to the specific needs of crisis-affected communities. This approach involves working closely with local stakeholders, including governments, communities, and beneficiaries themselves, to develop sustainable and culturally relevant educational solutions.

#### Focus on Specific Regions and Countries
ECW prioritizes regions and countries severely affected by crises, such as the Sahel, Central African Republic, Chad, South Sudan, Colombia, Yemen, and Syria. By focusing on these areas, ECW can allocate resources more efficiently and ensure that the most vulnerable populations receive the necessary support.

#### Financial Investment and Joint Programming
ECW invests in joint programming models, where multiple actors collaborate within a single program. This ensures that financial investments are leveraged effectively to achieve greater impact. For instance, ECW has been involved in projects that provide ongoing education support at the basic and primary levels in Yemen and has worked with the government of Chad to build schools for refugees from the Boko Haram crisis.

#### Rebuilding Schools and Providing Funding
In regions where schools have been damaged or destroyed due to conflict or natural disasters, ECW supports rebuilding efforts. For example, in Yemen, ECW has funded the rebuilding of schools lost to bombing tragedies, ensuring that children can continue their education despite challenging circumstances.

#### Emphasis on Inclusive Education
ECW advocates for inclusive education, ensuring that all children, regardless of their background, have access to quality education. This includes supporting programs for girls, people with disabilities, and other marginalized groups who are disproportionately affected by crises.

#### Leveraging Technology and Certification Mechanisms
ECW recognizes the potential of technology in overcoming physical barriers to education. During the COVID-19 pandemic, ECW supported the integration of digital tools and online platforms to ensure that students could continue learning remotely. Additionally, ECW promotes the globalization of certification mechanisms to ensure that educational credentials are recognized internationally, facilitating continued education and employment opportunities for displaced individuals.

#### Advocacy and Awareness Raising
Through high-level summits and press conferences, ECW raises awareness about the importance of education in crisis contexts. Events such as the Transforming Education Summit hosted by the UN Secretary-General highlight the urgency of educational support and advocate for increased financial commitments from donor countries.

#### Addressing Specific Challenges
ECW addresses the unique challenges faced by different regions. For instance, in Cameroon, ECW has responded to internet disruptions and controversies surrounding language policies in education. By addressing these specific issues, ECW ensures that its interventions are responsive to the diverse needs of crisis-affected communities.

#### Long-term Commitment
ECW is committed to providing long-term support, recognizing that crises can persist for extended periods. By maintaining a sustained presence and investing in resilience-building initiatives, ECW helps to prevent the loss of educational opportunities for generations of children and adolescents.

### Conclusion
Education Cannot Wait plays a pivotal role in bridging the gap between humanitarian aid and development assistance in crisis-affected contexts. Through strategic partnerships, co-creation, targeted investments, and innovative approaches, ECW ensures that education remains a priority and that children and adolescents can continue their learning journey despite the challenges posed by crises.
