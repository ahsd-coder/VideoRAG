### Role of Parents and Families in Supporting Children's Education and Fostering Lifelong Learning

#### Importance of Parental Involvement
Parental involvement plays a crucial role in supporting children's education and fostering lifelong learning. According to various sources, parents and families are fundamental in shaping a child's educational journey, influencing their attitudes toward learning, and providing the necessary support for academic success.

#### Parental Perceptions and Priorities
In Ghana, parents' perceptions of the purpose of education reveal that they prioritize civic and academic aspects more than others, as highlighted in a video presentation titled "YOUTH-LED MOBILIZATION DAY". Parents see education as a means to equip children with civic and academic skills, which aligns with their vision for their children’s future. However, there is a gap between what parents expect and what students experience currently, indicating areas where further engagement and understanding are needed.

#### Student Perspectives
Students in Ghana have different views on the purpose of education. They emphasize the importance of social, emotional, and employment skills, alongside academic achievements. This difference in perspective underscores the need for open dialogues between parents, educators, and students to align educational goals and ensure that the educational experience meets the diverse needs of learners.

#### Teacher-Student Disconnect
There is a notable disconnect between what teachers and students perceive as the most important purposes of education. Teachers often prioritize social-emotional learning, which surprises students, who assume that academics would be the primary focus. This discrepancy highlights the importance of continuous communication and collaboration among all stakeholders—parents, teachers, and students—to bridge these gaps and ensure a holistic educational approach.

#### Community Engagement
Educational initiatives benefit greatly from community involvement. Parents and families can play a vital role in advocating for and participating in educational reforms. For instance, the inclusion of young people in the decision-making processes regarding education in Côte d'Ivoire demonstrates the positive impact of community engagement and youth participation in shaping educational policies and practices.

#### Lifelong Learning
The concept of lifelong learning extends beyond formal education and includes continuous personal and professional development. Parents can foster a culture of lifelong learning by modeling curiosity, encouraging exploration, and valuing learning as an ongoing process. This mindset prepares children to adapt to an ever-changing world and embrace learning opportunities throughout their lives.

#### Overcoming Educational Crises
Addressing the global learning crisis requires concerted efforts from all sectors, including parents and families. The current situation, exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic, has highlighted the urgent need for innovative solutions and collaborative approaches to ensure that all children, especially those from marginalized communities, have access to quality education. Parents can contribute by advocating for educational reforms, supporting home learning initiatives, and engaging in community programs that promote literacy and numeracy skills.

#### Conclusion
In summary, the role of parents and families in supporting children's education and fostering lifelong learning is multifaceted and essential. By prioritizing civic and academic skills, engaging in open dialogues, bridging the teacher-student disconnect, promoting community involvement, and embracing the concept of lifelong learning, parents and families can significantly enhance educational outcomes and prepare children for a successful future.
