LLMs encounter significant challenges when dealing with tasks embedded in long texts due to context length limitations, difficulty with complex and technical content, inability to handle nuanced queries, failure to extract relevant information, and the need for simplified queries. Human intervention often becomes crucial in guiding these models through complex tasks and ensuring accurate responses.
