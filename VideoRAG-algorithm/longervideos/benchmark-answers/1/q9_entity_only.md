Question: How do the hunting strategies of arctic wolves and cheetahs differ when pursuing caribou?

### Hunting Strategies: Arctic Wolves vs Cheetahs

#### Arctic Wolves:
Arctic wolves primarily hunt caribou in a desolate, icy landscape. The video footage reveals several key aspects of their hunting strategy:

1. **Coordinated Effort**: Arctic wolves work together to corner and chase prey. This coordinated effort is evident as they move across snowy terrains in pursuit of caribou herds.
2. **Patience and Persistence**: They exhibit patience, stalking prey over vast distances for days until the opportunity arises. Once a target is identified, they maintain persistence, chasing tirelessly despite challenging weather conditions like fog or heavy snowfall.
3. **Adaptability to Terrain**: Arctic wolves are adept at navigating rugged terrains, using their sharp senses and agility to track down caribou across icy landscapes where hiding spots are scarce.

#### Cheetahs:
In contrast, cheetahs primarily operate in the African savanna when pursuing prey such as impalas. The hunting strategies of cheetahs include:

1. **Speed and Agility**: Cheetahs rely heavily on their remarkable speed—reaching up to 65 km/h—to chase down prey over short distances.
2. **Surprise Attacks**: They often employ ambush tactics, lying in wait for potential prey until the moment is right before launching a high-speed pursuit.
3. **Open Terrain Advantage**: Cheetahs prefer open plains where their speed can be maximized and where cover from trees or dense vegetation is minimal.

### Key Differences:
- **Environment**: Arctic wolves operate in harsh, icy conditions with few hiding spots, whereas cheetahs thrive in the expansive savanna.
- **Techniques Used**: While both species are effective hunters, arctic wolves use coordinated group efforts and persistence over long distances, while cheetahs leverage their unparalleled speed for quick, decisive strikes.

#### Summary:
The hunting strategies of Arctic wolves involve teamwork, patience, and adaptability to icy terrains, whereas cheetahs rely on swift bursts of speed in open savanna landscapes. Both species have evolved distinct methods suited to their unique environments and prey behaviors.
