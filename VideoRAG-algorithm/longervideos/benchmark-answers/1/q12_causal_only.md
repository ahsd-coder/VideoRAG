   - Fish populations have been driven closer to surface waters due to oxygen depletion caused by agricultural runoff. Eden’s whales take advantage of this by focusing their hunting efforts at shallower depths, where there is a higher concentration of fish.

   - The presence of humans and their activities in coastal areas has influenced whale behaviors. 5. **Response to Pollution-Driven Changes**:
   - Agricultural pollution leads to environmental changes that force marine life to adapt their hunting patterns. Eden's whales, like other large predators, now face challenges such as reduced prey availability and must find innovative ways to survive.
