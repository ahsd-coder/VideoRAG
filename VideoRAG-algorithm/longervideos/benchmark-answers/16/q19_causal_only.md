Question: What are the different visualization options available in SuperDecisions for displaying sensitivity analysis results?

### Visualization Options in SuperDecisions for Sensitivity Analysis

SuperDecisions offers several visualization options to help users effectively display and understand sensitivity analysis results. These options include:

1. **Bar Charts**:
   - **Description**: Bar charts allow users to compare values across different categories or alternatives.
   - **Example**: A bar chart can illustrate how changes in a criterion, such as prestige, affect the priorities of different car models (e.g., Acura TL, Toyota Camry, Honda Civic).

2. **Pie Charts**:
   - **Description**: Pie charts are useful for showing the proportion of each category relative to the total.
   - **Example**: A pie chart can depict the distribution of priorities among different alternatives based on a specific criterion.

3. **Horizontal Bar Charts**:
   - **Description**: Similar to standard bar charts, but oriented horizontally, these charts can be more readable when dealing with longer labels or categories.
   - **Example**: Horizontal bar charts can be used to compare the normalized priorities of various job alternatives under different criteria.

4. **Line Graphs**:
   - **Description**: Line graphs are effective for visualizing trends and changes over a range of values.
   - **Example**: A line graph can show how the priority of an alternative changes as the weight of a criterion (like prestige) varies.

5. **3D Bar Charts**:
   - **Description**: 3D bar charts provide a more visually engaging way to compare values across categories, though they can sometimes be harder to read accurately.
   - **Example**: A 3D bar chart can display the sensitivity of different car models to various criteria, such as price, comfort, and safety.

6. **Decision Tree Diagrams**:
   - **Description**: Decision tree diagrams can represent the hierarchical structure of criteria and alternatives, helping users understand the decision-making process.
   - **Example**: A decision tree can illustrate how changes in criteria weights affect the overall priorities of different alternatives in a structured manner.

### Steps to Use Visualization Tools in SuperDecisions

1. **Load Sample Models**: Start by loading a sample model, such as the "Card Choice" model, to practice sensitivity analysis.
   
2. **Navigate to Computations**: Access the "Computations" tab and select "Sensitivity" to begin the sensitivity analysis.

3. **Edit Independent Variables**: Adjust the independent variables to simulate different scenarios and observe the impact on the dependent variables.

4. **Select Visualization Type**: Choose from the available visualization options (bar charts, pie charts, line graphs, etc.) to display the sensitivity analysis results.

5. **Interpret Results**: Use the visualizations to interpret how changes in criteria weights affect the priorities of alternatives.

### Example Usage Scenarios

- **Car Selection Scenario**: Use sensitivity analysis to determine how changes in criteria such as price, comfort, and safety impact the priorities of different car models.
  
- **Job Selection Scenario**: Apply sensitivity analysis to evaluate how variations in criteria like salary, growth opportunities, and working environment influence the priorities of different job alternatives.

By leveraging these visualization options, users can gain deeper insights into how different factors affect their decision-making processes and make more informed choices.
