### Visualization of AHP Analysis Results in Super Decisions

#### Overview
The Super Decisions software provides several methods to visualize the results of Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) analysis, including the priorities of alternatives and criteria. These visualizations help users better understand the decision-making process and the relative importance of different elements within the model.

#### Key Steps and Features

1. **Synthesized Priorities**
   - **Step**: Navigate to the "Computations" tab and select "Synthesize the whole model."
   - **Result**: The software generates synthesized priorities for each alternative based on the criteria. These priorities are often displayed in a tabular format with columns for "Graphic," "Ideals," and "Normals."

2. **Graphical Representations**
   - **Bar Charts**: The software can display synthesized priorities using bar charts. Each bar represents the priority of an alternative, allowing for a quick comparison of the relative importance of each option.
   - **Example**: In a video tutorial, a bar chart is shown with bars labeled "Acura TL," "Toyota Camry," and "Honda Civic," each with corresponding numerical values representing their synthesized priorities.

3. **Network Diagrams**
   - **Step**: Use the "Network" tab to view the hierarchical structure of the decision-making model.
   - **Result**: Nodes representing criteria and alternatives are connected by lines, visually depicting the relationships and priorities between different elements.
   - **Example**: A video demonstrates a network diagram where the goal node is connected to criteria nodes like "Price," "Fuel Efficiency," and "Comfort," which are further connected to alternative nodes representing car models.

4. **Table Views**
   - **Step**: Access the "Ratings" tab to view detailed numerical data.
   - **Result**: Tables show raw data values, normalized values, and idealized column values, providing a comprehensive view of the decision-making process.
   - **Example**: A video showcases a table with columns for "Alternatives," "Prestige," "Driving Performance," "Space," and "Price," each containing numerical values that reflect the judgments entered during the AHP analysis.

5. **Limit Matrices**
   - **Step**: Use the "Computations" tab to generate limit matrices.
   - **Result**: These matrices provide a more detailed breakdown of how priorities were calculated, showing the contributions of each criterion to the overall priorities of alternatives.
   - **Example**: A video tutorial explains how to interpret the limit matrices, highlighting the final priorities for alternatives like "Acura TL," "Toyota Camry," and "Honda Civic."

6. **Sensitivity Analysis**
   - **Step**: Perform sensitivity analysis using the "Sensitivity" tab.
   - **Result**: This feature allows users to understand how changes in criteria weights affect the priorities of alternatives. Visualizations may include 3D bar charts or graphs showing the impact of varying criteria weights.
   - **Example**: A video showcases a 3D bar chart where the heights of bars representing alternatives change as the weight of a specific criterion is altered.

#### Conclusion
Super Decisions offers a variety of visualization tools to help users interpret the results of AHP analysis. From synthesized priorities displayed in bar charts to detailed network diagrams and limit matrices, these visualizations provide a comprehensive view of the decision-making process. Sensitivity analysis further enhances understanding by showing how changes in criteria weights impact the final priorities of alternatives.
