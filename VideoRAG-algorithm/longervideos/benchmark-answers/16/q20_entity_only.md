### Steps Involved in Connecting Criteria and Sub-Criteria to Alternatives in SuperDecisions

Connecting criteria and sub-criteria to alternatives in SuperDecisions involves a systematic process to ensure that all elements are properly linked within a hierarchical structure. Here's a detailed breakdown of the steps:

#### Step 1: Define the Goal and Criteria
1. **Open the Software Interface**:
   - Start by opening the SuperDecisions software and navigate to the main window.
   
2. **Set Up the Goal**:
   - Define the goal or main objective of your decision-making process. For example, if the goal is to select the best car, label the goal accordingly.
   
3. **Create Criteria Clusters**:
   - Create clusters for criteria. Common criteria might include "Prestige," "Price," "Miles Per Gallon (MPG)," and "Comfort."

#### Step 2: Add Sub-Criteria (Optional)
1. **Define Sub-Criteria**:
   - If applicable, create sub-criteria under each criterion. For instance, under "Comfort," you might have sub-criteria like "Driving Performance" and "Ride."

#### Step 3: Input Alternatives
1. **List Alternatives**:
   - List all the alternatives you are considering. For example, if you are choosing between car models, list models like "Acura TL," "Toyota Camry," and "Honda Civic."

#### Step 4: Establish Connections
1. **Connect Criteria to Alternatives**:
   - Begin by connecting each criterion to all alternatives. For example, connect "Prestige" to "Acura TL," "Toyota Camry," and "Honda Civic."
   
2. **Link Sub-Criteria to Alternatives**:
   - Similarly, link sub-criteria to the relevant alternatives. Ensure that each sub-criterion is connected to the appropriate alternatives under its parent criterion.

#### Step 5: Verify Connections
1. **Check Connections**:
   - Use the "Make/Show Connections" menu to verify that all connections have been made correctly. Highlight any missing connections and rectify them.

#### Step 6: Enter Judgments
1. **Input Judgment Values**:
   - Enter judgments for each connection. This involves assessing the importance or relevance of each criterion and sub-criterion relative to the alternatives. For example, determine how important "Prestige" is for "Acura TL" compared to other criteria.

#### Step 7: Synthesize Results
1. **Synthesize the Model**:
   - Once all judgments are entered, synthesize the model to compute the overall priorities. This step generates the final rankings or evaluations based on the criteria and alternatives.
