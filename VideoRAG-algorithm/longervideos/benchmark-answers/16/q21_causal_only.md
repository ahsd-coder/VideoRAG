Question: How can the 'inconsistency report' feature in SuperDecisions be used to identify and rectify inconsistencies in pairwise comparison judgments? 

### Using the 'Inconsistency Report' Feature in SuperDecisions

#### Identifying Inconsistencies
The 'Inconsistency Report' feature in SuperDecisions helps identify discrepancies in pairwise comparison judgments by highlighting areas of high inconsistency. When making pairwise comparisons, users assign numerical values to indicate the relative importance or preference between pairs of criteria. High inconsistency levels can arise when these judgments are not internally consistent.

**Steps to Identify Inconsistencies:**
1. **Navigate to the Judgments Section:** After entering pairwise comparisons, switch to the 'Judgments' tab or section within the software.
2. **Access the Inconsistency Report:** Click on the 'Inconsistency Report' option to generate a detailed report of all comparisons. This report typically includes a list of comparisons with their corresponding inconsistency values.
3. **Review the Report:** Examine the inconsistency values associated with each comparison. Values above a certain threshold (usually around 10%) indicate significant inconsistency and require attention.

#### Rectifying Inconsistencies
Once inconsistencies are identified, users can rectify them by adjusting the values of specific comparisons to improve overall consistency.

**Steps to Rectify Inconsistencies:**
1. **Adjust Values Based on Recommendations:** The inconsistency report often provides suggestions for adjustments. For example, it might recommend changing a value from 9 to 35 or from 7 to 2 to align with expected values and reduce inconsistency.
2. **Re-evaluate Judgments:** Consider revisiting the judgments that contribute significantly to the inconsistency. Reflect on the original criteria and reassess the relative importance or preference.
3. **Save Adjustments:** After making changes, ensure to save the adjusted judgments. The software will automatically recalculate the inconsistency values to reflect the new judgments.

#### Example Scenario
Consider a scenario where a user is comparing the importance of criteria such as 'Prestige' and 'Price' for a car selection decision. Initially, the inconsistency report might highlight a high inconsistency in the comparison between 'Prestige' and 'Price.' Upon reviewing the report, the user finds that adjusting the value from 9 to 35 improves the consistency significantly.

**Example Steps:**
1. **Identify Inconsistent Comparisons:** The report indicates that the comparison between 'Prestige' and 'Price' has an inconsistency value of 12%, which is above the recommended threshold.
2. **Adjust the Value:** Change the value from 9 to 35, as suggested by the report.
3. **Verify Changes:** Check the updated inconsistency report to confirm that the inconsistency has decreased to an acceptable level.

By systematically identifying and rectifying inconsistencies using the 'Inconsistency Report,' users can ensure that their pairwise comparison judgments are consistent and reliable, leading to more accurate and informed decision-making processes.
