### Steps Involved in Connecting Criteria and Sub-Criteria to Alternatives in SuperDecisions

Connecting criteria and sub-criteria to alternatives in SuperDecisions involves a structured process to ensure a clear hierarchy and proper linkage. Here are the detailed steps:

1. **Creating the Goal and Criteria Clusters:**
   - Start by creating a `Goal` cluster to define the overarching objective or decision you're making.
   - Add `Criteria` clusters representing the major factors or dimensions that influence your decision. For example, in a car selection scenario, criteria might include `Prestige`, `Price`, and `Comfort`.

2. **Adding Sub-Criteria:**
   - Within each `Criteria` cluster, you can create `Sub-Criteria` to break down the criteria into more specific factors. For instance, under `Comfort`, you might have `Driving Performance` and `Ride`.
   - Ensure that sub-criteria are connected to their respective parent criteria. This can be done through the software's interface by selecting the appropriate nodes and connecting them.

3. **Setting Up the Alternatives Cluster:**
   - Create an `Alternatives` cluster to list the options or alternatives you are considering. For example, if you are choosing between different car models, each model would be an alternative.
   - Add each alternative to the `Alternatives` cluster, ensuring they are numbered or named appropriately for easy reference.

4. **Linking Criteria and Sub-Criteria to Alternatives:**
   - Navigate to the `Connections` tab or section within SuperDecisions.
   - Use the `Make/Show Connections` feature to establish links between criteria/sub-criteria and alternatives.
   - Highlight the criteria/sub-criteria nodes and select the alternatives to connect them. For example, you might connect `Prestige` to each car model (alternative) to evaluate how prestigious each car is.

5. **Reviewing and Adjusting Connections:**
   - After establishing the initial connections, review them to ensure that all criteria and sub-criteria are correctly linked to the alternatives.
   - Adjust any incorrect connections by breaking and re-establishing links as needed.

6. **Entering Judgments:**
   - Once the connections are set, proceed to enter judgments for each criterion/sub-criterion against the alternatives.
   - Use the `Judgments` tab or feature to input your assessments. This might involve pairwise comparisons, rating scales, or other methods depending on the complexity of the decision.

7. **Synthesizing Results:**
   - After entering judgments, synthesize the results to obtain a comprehensive evaluation of each alternative based on the criteria and sub-criteria.
   - Review the synthesized priorities to identify the best alternative according to the established hierarchy and judgments.

By following these steps, you can effectively connect criteria and sub-criteria to alternatives in SuperDecisions, ensuring a structured and logical hierarchy for your decision-making process.
