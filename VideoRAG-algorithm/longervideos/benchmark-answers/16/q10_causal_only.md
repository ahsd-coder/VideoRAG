Question: Describe the two phases involved in implementing absolute measurement in SuperDecisions software.

### Overview of Implementing Absolute Measurement in SuperDecisions Software

Implementing absolute measurement in the SuperDecisions software involves a structured process designed to evaluate and rank alternatives based on specified criteria. The process is broken down into two distinct phases, each serving a crucial role in the overall evaluation framework.

#### Phase 1: Setting Up the Model and Criteria

**Objective:** Establish the foundational structure of the decision-making model, including the criteria and alternatives.

**Steps Involved:**

1. **Define Criteria:** Identify and categorize the criteria that will be used to evaluate the alternatives. For example, in the context of MBA admissions, criteria might include Academic Track Record (ATR), Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA), Work Experience (WE), Letters of Recommendation (LOR), and Extracurricular Activities (EA).

2. **Add Alternatives:** Input the list of alternatives or candidates that will be evaluated against the established criteria. In the case of MBA admissions, this would involve listing the names of the candidates.

3. **Establish Hierarchical Structure:** Organize the criteria and alternatives into a hierarchical structure within the software. This helps in systematically comparing and evaluating the alternatives based on the criteria.

4. **Define Rating Scales:** For each criterion, define the rating scales that will be used to assess the alternatives. This ensures a standardized approach to evaluating the performance of each alternative against the criteria.

#### Phase 2: Evaluating and Synthesizing Results

**Objective:** Perform the actual evaluation and synthesis of results to determine the final rankings or scores for each alternative.

**Steps Involved:**

1. **Input Judgments:** Enter judgments or ratings for each alternative under each criterion. This involves assessing how well each candidate meets the criteria established in Phase 1.

2. **Synthesize Results:** Use the software's functionalities to synthesize the judgments and generate overall scores or rankings for each alternative. This step combines the individual judgments into a comprehensive evaluation.

3. **Review and Validate:** Review the synthesized results to ensure consistency and accuracy. Adjustments may be necessary to address any inconsistencies or errors in the judgments.

4. **Final Rankings:** Generate the final rankings or scores for each alternative based on the synthesized results. This provides a clear and objective basis for making decisions.

### Example Application: MBA Admissions Selection

In the context of MBA admissions, the two phases can be applied as follows:

- **Phase 1:** Define criteria such as ATR, CGPA, WE, LOR, and EA. Add the list of candidates. Establish a hierarchical structure reflecting the importance of each criterion. Define rating scales for each criterion.
  
- **Phase 2:** Input judgments for each candidate against each criterion. Synthesize the judgments to produce overall scores. Review and validate the results. Generate final rankings for the candidates based on the synthesized scores.

By following these two phases, the SuperDecisions software enables a systematic and rigorous approach to decision-making, ensuring that all relevant factors are considered and evaluated objectively.
