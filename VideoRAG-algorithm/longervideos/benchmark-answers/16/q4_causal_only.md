Question: How can the global priorities of alternatives be calculated in AHP using Super Decisions?

### Calculating Global Priorities of Alternatives Using AHP in Super Decisions

To calculate the global priorities of alternatives using the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) in Super Decisions, follow these steps:

#### Step 1: Set Up Your Model
1. **Define Goals and Criteria:** Begin by defining your goal node and the criteria that will be used to evaluate the alternatives. This can be done through the "Main Network" or "Cluster" tabs, depending on the complexity of your decision-making scenario.
   
2. **Input Data:** Input the necessary data for pairwise comparisons and ratings. This involves comparing criteria against each other and assigning priorities based on their relative importance.

#### Step 2: Perform Pairwise Comparisons
1. **Select Nodes for Comparison:** Use the "Judgments" tab to select nodes and make pairwise comparisons based on the criteria you have defined. Ensure that all comparisons are consistent, with an acceptable consistency ratio (CR).

2. **Adjust Consistency Ratio:** If the consistency ratio is high, adjust the comparisons to improve consistency. This may involve revisiting some of the comparisons and making minor adjustments to the ratings.

#### Step 3: Synthesize Local Priorities
1. **Compute Local Priorities:** Once all comparisons are completed, click on the "Computations" tab to compute the local priorities for each alternative based on the criteria. This step synthesizes the local priorities into a single value for each alternative.

#### Step 4: Compute Global Priorities
1. **Access Synthesize Option:** Navigate to the "Synthesize" option within the "Computations" tab to compute the global priorities. This involves combining the local priorities with the criteria priorities to derive the overall global priorities for each alternative.

2. **View Results:** After synthesizing, the software will display the global priorities for each alternative. These priorities represent the overall ranking of alternatives based on the defined criteria.

#### Step 5: Verify and Adjust
1. **Review Results:** Check the synthesized priorities to ensure they align with your expectations. If necessary, revisit the comparisons and ratings to refine the results.

2. **Save and Export:** Save your model and export the results if needed. You can use the "File" menu to save your model or export the data to other formats for further analysis.

### Example Workflow
- **Initial Setup:**
  - Open the Super Decisions software and navigate to the "Main Network" tab.
  - Define your goal node and criteria.
  
- **Pairwise Comparisons:**
  - Go to the "Judgments" tab and make pairwise comparisons for the criteria.
  - Ensure consistency ratios are within acceptable limits (typically CR < 0.1).

- **Local Priorities Calculation:**
  - Switch to the "Computations" tab and select "Compute Local Priorities."
  - Confirm that the local priorities are correctly calculated.

- **Global Priorities Calculation:**
  - Use the "Synthesize" option under "Computations" to compute global priorities.
  - View the synthesized priorities for each alternative.

- **Final Review:**
  - Review the synthesized priorities and make any necessary adjustments.
  - Save and export the results as needed.

By following these steps, you can effectively calculate the global priorities of alternatives using AHP in Super Decisions, ensuring a structured and methodical approach to decision-making.
