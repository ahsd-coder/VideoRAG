### How the 'Self-Advance' System Enhances Player Combat Abilities

The **Self-Advance** system within these games is a critical feature that allows players to customize and improve their characters in numerous ways, significantly enhancing their combat capabilities. Here’s how it works:

1. **Skill Trees**: The Self-Advance menu typically includes several skill trees such as Stamina, Martial Arts, Survival, Staff Stances, Mysticism, Alteration, Strand, and Transformations. Each tree offers unique abilities that can be unlocked or upgraded using Sparks (a form of currency), providing players with multiple paths to enhance their combat effectiveness.

2. **Upgrade Options**: Players can focus on specific areas like defense, attack, or survival by selecting appropriate talents within the skill trees. For example:
   - **Defense**: Upgrading skills such as "Rough Skin" increases maximum stamina and reduces debuff durations.
   - **Attack**: Enhancing abilities like "Focused Attack II," which increases Focus Points from one to two, allowing more powerful strikes.

3. **Strategic Decisions**: The system encourages players to make strategic decisions based on their playstyle. For instance, choosing between offensive or defensive upgrades depending on the combat scenario or enemy types.
   - Players can decide whether to prioritize abilities like "Mobile Spin" for increased evasion or "Spreading Fire" for area damage.

4. **Stamina Management**: The Stamina tree within Staff Stances emphasizes managing stamina effectively during combat scenarios, ensuring players have enough energy for crucial moves and defense mechanisms.
   - Upgrading "Grieved Body," which significantly reduces the stamina cost of charged attacks, provides a strategic advantage in high-intensity battles.

5. **Relic Selection**: The Self-Advance system also includes relic selections that can drastically alter character performance by enhancing core stats like health or damage output.
   - Selecting relics such as "Longstrand" to increase duplicate duration at higher talent levels, offering flexibility and adaptability during combat.

6. **Flexibility and Customization**: The ability to swap out relics or change focus points allows players to adjust their character's capabilities dynamically, adapting to different situations or enemy types.
   - Players can experiment with various combinations to find the most effective setup for their preferred playstyle.

The Self-Advance system is crucial for players looking to maximize their combat effectiveness.
