Question: How does the player acquire new spells?

### Acquiring New Spells

Players can acquire new spells through several methods within the game:

1. **Spell Configuration Menu:**
   - Players can configure their spells via a menu, which allows them to set and change active spells for combat.

2. **Relics and Talents:**
   - Certain relics or talents can be upgraded at the "Self-Advance" menu.
   - For example, enhancing abilities like "Grieved Body," which reduces stamina costs for charged attacks and increases health.

3. **Skill Trees and Progression:**
   - Players progress through skill trees such as "Foundation," "Staff Stances," "Mysticism," and others.
   - Each branch contains various talents that can be upgraded, often unlocking new spells or enhancing existing ones.

4. **Spell Books and Items:**
   - Some spells may be acquired by finding spell books or items within the game world.
   - For instance, collecting materials like "Sea-Supporting Tendon" and activating spells such as "Golden Thunder."

5. **Interactive Environments:**
   - Players can interact with specific environments or objects to unlock new abilities and spells.
   - For example, using spell binders at shrines or breaking pots for additional will points.

6. **Progression through Chapters:**
   - Advancing through chapters often reveals new spells and unlocks previously unavailable options.
   - Completing quests or achieving milestones may also grant access to new spells.

7. **Exploration and Resource Management:**
   - Exploring different areas, collecting materials, and managing resources can lead to discovering new spells and enhancing existing ones.
   - For instance, navigating through a snowy landscape or encountering mystical shrines might yield new spell options.

### Example Spells

- **Golden Thunder:** A deadly dance that transforms into Yellow Loong with immunity to shock and ability to see through enemies.
- **Immobilize:** This control effect can bind the enemy in place, providing an advantage during combat.

By following these steps and engaging deeply within the game's mechanics, players can unlock a variety of new spells to enhance their character’s abilities.
