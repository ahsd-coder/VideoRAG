Question: How does Bezos's view of competition shape his approach to business and innovation?

### How Jeff Bezos Views Competition and Its Impact on Business and Innovation

#### Understanding Bezos's Perspective on Competition

Jeff Bezos, the founder and CEO of Amazon, has a nuanced view of competition that significantly shapes his approach to business and innovation. According to interviews and discussions at events like the New York Times DealBook Summit, Bezos acknowledges the importance of strong competition. He views competition as a driving force for improvement and innovation, rather than a threat to be feared or avoided.

#### Emphasis on Innovation Over Direct Competition

Bezos believes that competition should not be seen as a zero-sum game. Instead, he focuses on innovation and building long-term value. This philosophy is reflected in his approach to Amazon, where he encourages a culture of continuous improvement and innovation. For example, Bezos has spoken about the importance of being early in developing new technologies and services, such as AI, rather than waiting for competitors to set the standard.

#### Example from the AWS Founding Story

One anecdote from the DealBook Summit highlights this perspective. Bezos mentioned visiting a brewery in Luxembourg that had been operating for 300 years. This experience inspired the founding of AWS (Amazon Web Services), illustrating how Bezos draws inspiration from long-standing institutions to innovate and differentiate Amazon's offerings. He sees the longevity of these institutions as a testament to their ability to adapt and innovate over time.

#### Leadership and Team Dynamics

Bezos places significant emphasis on the quality of leadership and team dynamics. During discussions at the summit, he stressed the importance of hiring "missionaries" rather than "mercenaries"—individuals who are passionate about their work and committed to building something meaningful rather than simply seeking short-term gains. This mindset fosters a collaborative and innovative environment within Amazon.

#### Risk Capital and Entrepreneurial Spirit

At the DealBook Summit, Bezos also discussed the importance of a robust risk capital system in fostering innovation. He noted that the United States has a unique advantage due to its strong venture capital ecosystem, which enables entrepreneurs to secure funding for high-risk, high-reward projects. This supportive environment encourages continuous innovation and drives competitive advancements in technology and business models.

#### Conclusion

In summary, Jeff Bezos's view of competition is deeply intertwined with his approach to business and innovation. Rather than fearing competition, Bezos embraces it as a catalyst for growth and innovation. By fostering a culture of continuous improvement, hiring passionate leaders, and leveraging a supportive ecosystem for risk-taking, Bezos ensures that Amazon remains at the forefront of technological and business innovation.
