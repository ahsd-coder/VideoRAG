Question: Describe Bezos's belief in the importance of developing the 'next generation of leaders'.

### Jeff Bezos's Belief in Developing the Next Generation of Leaders

At The New York Times DealBook Summit, Jeff Bezos emphasized the importance of developing the next generation of leaders within organizations. During a discussion, he articulated three core responsibilities of leaders:

1. **Identifying Big Ideas**: Leaders must be capable of identifying significant and transformative concepts that can drive the organization forward.
2. **Enforcing Tough Execution**: Once the big ideas are identified, leaders need to ensure that these ideas are executed with rigor and determination.
3. **Growing the Next Generation of Leaders**: Bezos stressed the necessity of nurturing and cultivating future leaders within the organization.

Bezos highlighted the significance of the third responsibility, stating that growing the next generation of leaders is critical. He explained that his role currently involves ensuring that Andy Jassy, the leader of the Amazon Web Services (AWS) division, continues to excel and grow. Bezos expressed pride in the team's rapid innovation and effective management, indicating that their success contributes to the overall strength of Amazon.

This emphasis on leadership development aligns with Bezos's broader vision for Amazon. He aims to build a company that will outlast him, ensuring that it continues to thrive under capable leadership. By fostering a culture of mentorship and continuous learning, Bezos hopes to maintain Amazon's innovative spirit and competitive edge well into the future.
