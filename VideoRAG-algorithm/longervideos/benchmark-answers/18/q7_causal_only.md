Question: What industry does Elon Musk compare the production of humanoid robots to in terms of global capacity?

### Comparison of Production Capacities

Elon Musk compares the production of humanoid robots to the automobile industry in terms of global capacity. Specifically, he draws parallels between the current global capacity for vehicle production and what might be required for mass-producing humanoid robots.

#### Global Vehicle Production Capacity
- **Current Capacity**: The global capacity for producing vehicles is approximately 100 million units annually.
- **Demand**: There is a current demand for around 100 million vehicles per year.
- **In Use**: Roughly 2 billion vehicles are currently in use globally.

#### Mass Production of Humanoid Robots
- **Comparison**: Musk suggests that the production capabilities for humanoid robots could mirror those of the automotive industry, implying that a similar level of production—potentially upwards of 100 million units annually—could be achievable.
- **Utility**: He mentions that the utility of humanoid robots is significantly higher, hinting that the demand for such robots could surpass that of vehicles, potentially reaching a billion or more units per year.

This comparison underscores the potential scale and feasibility of mass-producing humanoid robots, drawing on the established infrastructure and capacity of the automotive industry.
