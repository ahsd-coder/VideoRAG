This method leverages mathematical principles to efficiently handle the complexities of image rotations, facilitating the accurate placement of fragments within the original fresco.

#### Application in Restoration

Using circular harmonics, they can identify and correctly position these fragments despite their rotations. Historically, the restoration of frescoes relied heavily on manual methods, which were labor-intensive and prone to errors. With the advent of digital techniques, the process has become more systematic and precise. The Mantegna Project (2001-2004) exemplifies this transition, involving collaborations between various academic disciplines to develop computational methods for fragment analysis and reconstruction.
