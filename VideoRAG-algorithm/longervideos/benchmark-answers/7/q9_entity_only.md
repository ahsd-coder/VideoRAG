Question: What motivations did Ingrid Daubechies express for engaging in collaborations with art historians and conservators?

### Motivations for Collaboration with Art Historians and Conservators

Ingrid Daubechies expressed several motivations for engaging in collaborations with art historians and conservators:

1. **Applying Mathematical Techniques to Art Conservation**:
   - Daubechies highlighted her interest in applying mathematical algorithms to assist in the conservation and analysis of artworks. For instance, she discussed the use of mathematical tools for image processing and analysis in art conservation, as seen in her work with the Van Gogh Museum.

2. **Reconstructing Damaged Artworks**:
   - One notable example is her involvement in the Ghissi Project, a collaboration between Duke University and the North Carolina Museum of Art, which focused on virtually aging and rejuvenating paintings. This project demonstrates her commitment to using mathematical techniques to reconstruct and restore damaged artworks, enhancing their preservation.

3. **Contributing to Art Historical Discoveries**:
   - Through her work, Daubechies contributed to significant discoveries in art history. For example, she mentioned how her team’s analysis of historical texts revealed new insights, such as recognizing more letters and groups of words in illegible manuscripts, leading to a deeper understanding of the content.

4. **Educational Purposes**:
   - Daubechies discussed the creation of high-quality art reproductions for educational purposes, often commissioned by museums. These reproductions serve to educate the public and provide a closer look at masterpieces that might otherwise be inaccessible.

5. **Interdisciplinary Research**:
   - She emphasized the importance of interdisciplinary research, bringing together mathematicians, art historians, and conservators to tackle complex problems in art conservation and analysis. This collaboration not only enriches the field of mathematics but also provides valuable insights for art historians and conservators.

6. **Preserving Cultural Heritage**:
   - Daubechies stressed the role of mathematics in preserving cultural heritage by enabling detailed examination and restoration of historical artifacts. This includes virtually aging and rejuvenating paintings, which helps in understanding and documenting the evolution of artworks over time.

7. **Advancing Scientific Methods**:
   - By integrating mathematical methodologies into art conservation, Daubechies aimed to advance scientific methods used in the field. This includes the development of new tools and techniques for analyzing and restoring artworks, thereby pushing the boundaries of both mathematics and art history.

These motivations underscore Daubechies' dedication to leveraging her expertise in mathematics to contribute meaningfully to the preservation and study of art, highlighting the potential for cross-disciplinary collaboration to yield significant advancements in both fields.
