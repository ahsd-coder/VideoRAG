Question: What motivations did Ingrid Daubechies express for engaging in collaborations with art historians and conservators?

### Motivations for Engaging in Collaborations with Art Historians and Conservators

Ingrid Daubechies expressed several motivations for engaging in collaborations with art historians and conservators:

1. **Advancing Scientific Techniques in Art Preservation**:
   - Daubechies and her team developed mathematical algorithms to aid in the preservation and analysis of artworks. For instance, they used these algorithms to help piece together fragments of frescoes destroyed during World War II, virtually remove artifacts for restoration campaigns, and reveal hidden details beneath visible paintings. This demonstrates her commitment to applying advanced scientific techniques to enhance the field of art conservation.

2. **Addressing Complex Art Restoration Challenges**:
   - She mentioned specific examples where mathematical algorithms played a critical role, such as the Ghissi Project, which involved virtually aging and rejuvenating paintings on panel. This project highlights her motivation to tackle intricate restoration challenges that require sophisticated computational methods.

3. **Enhancing Understanding and Authenticity of Artwork**:
   - Daubechies collaborated with institutions like the Van Gogh Museum to develop tools for distinguishing authentic artworks from copies and forgeries. This underscores her aim to improve the accuracy and authenticity of art attributions through rigorous scientific analysis.

4. **Educational and Public Engagement**:
   - She discussed the creation of virtual reproductions of artworks to provide a historical perspective and educate the public. By recreating lost artwork panels, as seen in the work of Charlotte Gaspers, she aimed to offer educational resources that could enhance public understanding and appreciation of art history.

5. **Interdisciplinary Collaboration**:
   - Daubechies emphasized the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration, working closely with art historians, conservators, and mathematicians. This collaboration ensures that the methodologies employed are both scientifically sound and practically applicable, bridging the gap between theoretical advancements and real-world applications in art conservation.

6. **Respect for Artistic Integrity**:
   - During her presentations, she highlighted the need to balance automation with human oversight in restoration processes. For example, she noted that while computer algorithms can identify potential matches, human operators play a crucial role in verifying these selections. This reflects her respect for the nuanced decision-making required in art conservation and her desire to integrate human expertise with technological advancements.

These motivations illustrate Daubechies' multifaceted approach to integrating mathematics with art history and conservation, driven by a combination of scientific curiosity, practical necessity, and a commitment to preserving cultural heritage.
