Question: What mathematical concept, used to analyze signal frequency, did Daubechies employ to analyze variations in scale and detail within Van Gogh's brushstrokes?

### Mathematical Concept Used by Daubechies for Signal Frequency Analysis

Daubechies utilized the concept of **wavelet decomposition** to analyze variations in scale and detail within Van Gogh's brushstrokes. This technique allows for the examination of information locally in a painting but at different scales, effectively breaking down complex signals into simpler components to understand their frequency variations.

#### Wavelet Decomposition Process

1. **Blurring and Digitization**: The process begins by blurring the original image of Van Gogh's work, such as the clogs painting, to simulate different levels of detail. When the image is blurred slightly, it results in a reduction of fine details. By digitizing the blurred image, it converts into a set of numerical values that can be analyzed.

2. **Analyzing Local Details**: Wavelet decomposition enables the analysis of local details at varying resolutions. This method helps in isolating and examining specific features at different scales, revealing how fine details emerge and disappear as the image is progressively blurred or sharpened.

3. **Visualizing Differences**: After digitizing and blurring the image, the differences between the original and blurred versions are calculated and visualized. These differences highlight the variations in brushstroke detail and texture. 

4. **Reconstructing Details**: Through wavelet analysis, lost details can be reconstructed and visualized, enhancing the understanding of the original artwork's complexity and the artist's technique.

#### Application in Art Analysis

- **Van Gogh's Clogs**: In the presentation at the International Congress of Mathematicians, Daubechies demonstrated wavelet decomposition using Van Gogh's clogs painting. By progressively blurring the image and analyzing the resulting numerical data, she illustrated how wavelet decomposition can capture and visualize variations in brushstrokes and textures.

- **Edge Detection and Pattern Recognition**: The technique also facilitates the identification of edges and patterns within the painting. As seen in the video presentations, Daubechies used wavelet features to detect edges and analyze patterns in the paintings, providing insights into the artist's unique style and brushwork.

By leveraging wavelet decomposition, Daubechies was able to dissect Van Gogh's brushstrokes at various scales, offering a deeper understanding of the artist's technique and the intricate details embedded in his work.
