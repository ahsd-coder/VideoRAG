### Role of 'Blend' in Sora

**Definition and Functionality:** 
The 'Blend' feature in Sora allows users to combine two different video scenes into a cohesive new scene. Essentially, it takes two input videos and creates a seamless blend of both, merging them into a new, unified video that maintains the visual coherence of both inputs.

**Process:**
When using Blend, users can specify two scenes or videos that they want to merge. Sora then analyzes these inputs and creates a new video that smoothly transitions from one scene to the other, making it seem as though the two originally separate scenes are part of the same continuous narrative. This process can involve adjusting timings, transitions, and visual elements to ensure a natural flow between the two inputs.

   - **Consistent Aesthetics:** Users can maintain consistent aesthetics and styles across different segments of a video, ensuring that the final output feels cohesive and professionally produced.

3. **Advanced Editing Capabilities:**
