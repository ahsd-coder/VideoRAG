Question: What specific measures has the FIA taken to reduce the cost of entry-level motorsports, such as karting and cross-car racing?

### Measures Taken by FIA to Reduce Entry-Level Motorsports Costs

The FIA has implemented several initiatives aimed at reducing the cost of entry-level motorsports, specifically focusing on karting and cross-car racing. These measures are designed to increase accessibility and inclusivity within the sport, ensuring that more individuals can participate without prohibitive financial barriers.

#### 1. **Cross-Car Initiative**
One of the key initiatives is the introduction of the cross-car program. This program aims to provide a blueprint with FIA safety standards for cross-car and karting activities. By establishing a standardized approach, the FIA ensures that these vehicles meet rigorous safety criteria while also reducing the overall cost for participants. This blueprint serves as a template for regional manufacturing, enabling local production and reducing transportation and import costs.

#### 2. **Blueprint Distribution**
The FIA has committed to distributing these blueprints to various regions, ensuring that the guidelines are widely available. This effort is intended to facilitate the creation of locally manufactured cross-cars and karts, thereby lowering the initial investment required for aspiring drivers. 

#### 3. **Affordability Emphasis**
The FIA has highlighted the affordability aspect of karting, positioning it as the grassroots of Formula One. They emphasize that karting is significantly more cost-effective compared to other forms of motorsport. For instance, they mention that karting costs 75% less than other entry-level options, making it a viable starting point for many young racers.

#### 4. **Support for Emerging Talent**
The FIA has also shown support for emerging talent by congratulating technical schools and communities that have successfully produced cross-cars and karts. Notable achievements, such as the first-ever cross-car built in Rwanda by students from a technical school, demonstrate the tangible impact of these initiatives. These successes serve as inspiration and motivation for other regions to follow suit.

#### 5. **Celebration of Achievements**
During formal events, such as the FIA Awards, the FIA celebrates these achievements and milestones, reinforcing the importance of these initiatives. By doing so, they not only recognize the efforts of those involved but also encourage continued investment and support from various stakeholders in the motorsport community.

In summary, the FIA's measures to reduce the cost of entry-level motorsports include the introduction of standardized blueprints, emphasis on affordability, support for emerging talent, and celebration of achievements. These steps collectively aim to make motorsports more accessible and inclusive, encouraging greater participation at the grassroots level.
