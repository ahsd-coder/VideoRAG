Synchrosqueezing can help map these variations geographically, offering insights into how birds adapt their vocalizations based on environmental factors and social interactions.

Synchrosqueezing can reveal the learning process by analyzing how closely juvenile birds mimic adult calls.    - **Brain Function**: Studying bird songs using synchrosqueezing can provide clues about the neurological processes involved in vocalization and learning.
