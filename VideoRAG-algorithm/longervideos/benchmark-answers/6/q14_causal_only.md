### Medical Application of Time-Frequency Analysis in Fetal Health

In the context of fetal health, Daubechies highlights the use of time-frequency analysis for monitoring fetal heartbeats non-invasively. Specifically, the application involves analyzing electrocardiogram (ECG) signals collected from a pregnant mother to extract the fetal heartbeat. This method is advantageous compared to invasive techniques that require placing a probe inside the mother's body, which can pose risks and discomfort.

#### Key Points:

1. **Non-Invasive Monitoring**:
   - Traditional methods of monitoring fetal heartbeats often involve invasive procedures, such as placing a probe inside the mother’s body, which can be risky and uncomfortable.
   - Signal analysis techniques, particularly time-frequency analysis, offer a safer and more comfortable alternative.

2. **Signal Processing Techniques**:
   - Time-frequency analysis techniques, including Daubechies wavelets, are employed to isolate and analyze the fetal heartbeat from the mother's ECG signal.
   - These techniques help in distinguishing the fetal heartbeat from the dominant maternal heartbeat, which is crucial for accurate diagnosis.

3. **Clinical Relevance**:
   - Extracting the fetal heartbeat through signal analysis is particularly valuable for diagnosing potential heart problems in fetuses.
   - This method allows for continuous monitoring of fetal heart health during pregnancy, especially for fetuses suspected of having heart issues.

4. **Technical Details**:
   - Electrocardiogram (ECG) signals are recorded from electrodes placed around the navel and pubic symphysis of the pregnant mother.
   - The signals are processed using time-frequency analysis to identify the fetal heartbeat amidst the maternal heartbeat and other noise.

By leveraging time-frequency analysis, medical professionals can effectively monitor fetal heart health without the need for invasive procedures, thereby improving diagnostic accuracy and patient comfort.
