Question: What is the role of lead qualification in an AI-powered email assistant, and how is it implemented?

### Role of Lead Qualification in an AI-Powered Email Assistant

Lead qualification is crucial in an AI-powered email assistant because it helps determine the priority and relevance of leads based on predefined criteria. This process ensures that the most promising leads receive timely and appropriate attention, thereby improving conversion rates and sales efficiency. In the context of an AI assistant, lead qualification can be automated to analyze incoming emails and categorize them according to specific criteria, such as intent, urgency, or potential value.

### Implementation of Lead Qualification in an AI-Powered Email Assistant

The implementation of lead qualification in an AI-powered email assistant involves several steps:

1. **Data Collection**: The assistant gathers data from past emails and replies to build a knowledge base. This data includes information such as the sender's intent, the nature of the inquiry, and previous interactions.
