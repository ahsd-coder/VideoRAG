### Reorganization Efforts by Saint-Gobain to Address Sustainability Challenges

#### Strategic Shifts and Organizational Changes

Saint-Gobain, a leading company in the building materials industry, has undergone significant reorganization to address sustainability challenges. According to the presentations given by the company's executives at Columbia University, Saint-Gobain restructured itself to better tackle environmental issues and promote sustainable practices. Here are the key points:

1. **Transition from Product Lines to Country Divisions**:
   - In a major organizational shift, Saint-Gobain transformed its structure from being organized by product lines to being organized by country divisions. This change aimed to streamline operations and enhance the company's ability to provide comprehensive solutions tailored to local needs.
   - By reorganizing into country divisions, Saint-Gobain can more effectively address the unique challenges and opportunities presented by different regions, thereby improving its overall impact on sustainability.

2. **Focus on Circular Economy and Recycling**:
   - Saint-Gobain has embraced the concept of a circular economy, focusing on recycling materials back into their manufacturing processes. This approach not only reduces waste but also enhances the efficiency and sustainability of their operations.
   - For instance, the company has implemented initiatives to recycle demolition waste and reintegrate it into manufacturing processes, demonstrating a commitment to closed-loop systems.

3. **Internal Carbon Pricing**:
   - To further minimize their environmental footprint, Saint-Gobain introduced an internal carbon price since 2016. This mechanism helps the company account for the cost of carbon emissions internally, encouraging departments and subsidiaries to adopt more sustainable practices.
   - By imposing this internal carbon price, Saint-Gobain aims to drive reductions in CO2 emissions and improve energy efficiency across its operations.

4. **Investment in Sustainable Solutions**:
   - The company has invested heavily in developing sustainable building materials and solutions. For example, they have launched initiatives aimed at reducing CO2 emissions and improving energy efficiency in buildings.
   - Saint-Gobain’s strategy involves providing solutions that reduce energy bills by up to 70%, thereby contributing to a more sustainable built environment.

5. **Global Leadership in Light and Sustainable Construction**:
   - Saint-Gobain aims to become a leader in light and sustainable construction, focusing on reducing the carbon footprint of buildings and improving their overall sustainability.
   - This strategic direction aligns with the company’s purpose of making the world a better place, emphasizing the importance of sustainable practices in their operations and product offerings.

6. **Commitment to Continuous Improvement**:
   - Saint-Gobain is committed to continuous improvement in sustainability. They monitor their progress regularly, setting ambitious targets for reducing CO2 emissions and improving energy efficiency.
   - The company has achieved significant reductions in CO2 emissions, with a 27% drop in total emissions over five years, while simultaneously growing their turnover.

By implementing these structural and operational changes, Saint-Gobain demonstrates a strong commitment to sustainability, aiming to reduce their environmental impact while fostering sustainable practices in the construction industry.
