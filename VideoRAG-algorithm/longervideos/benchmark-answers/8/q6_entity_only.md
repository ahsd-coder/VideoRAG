Question: What environmental challenges does Saint-Gobain address in the built environment?

### Environmental Challenges Addressed by Saint-Gobain in the Built Environment

#### Overview
Saint-Gobain, a global leader in sustainable construction, addresses several significant environmental challenges within the built environment. These challenges encompass areas such as carbon emissions, energy efficiency, natural resource preservation, and urbanization. The company employs a multifaceted approach to tackle these issues, leveraging innovative technologies and sustainable practices.

#### Carbon Emissions
One of the primary challenges Saint-Gobain addresses is the substantial contribution of the built environment to global CO2 emissions. According to the CEO's presentation at Columbia University, the built environment is responsible for approximately 37-40% of global CO2 emissions. To mitigate this, Saint-Gobain implements various strategies:

- **Internal Carbon Pricing**: Since 2016, the company has imposed an internal carbon price on capital expenditures and research and development projects to account for the increasing costs of carbon taxes.
  
- **Low-Carbon Solutions**: Saint-Gobain develops and promotes low-carbon building materials and solutions. For instance, they launched low-carbon glass products that significantly reduce CO2 emissions.

#### Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting
Energy efficiency is a critical focus area for Saint-Gobain. Retrofitting existing buildings to enhance energy performance is essential given that a majority of buildings in existence today will still be in use in 2050. Key initiatives include:

- **Energy Efficiency Renovations**: Saint-Gobain works on improving the energy efficiency of existing buildings through renovations and upgrades.
  
- **Passive House Innovations**: The company contributes to the development of passive house standards, which aim to drastically reduce energy consumption in buildings.

#### Natural Resource Preservation
Preserving natural resources is another key challenge addressed by Saint-Gobain. The company recognizes that current practices are unsustainable and seeks to implement more responsible methods:

- **Circular Economy Initiatives**: Saint-Gobain promotes a circular economy by utilizing recycled materials in their products. For example, their glass wool insulation products contain up to 70% recycled materials, and they produce plasterboard with up to 50% recycled content.

- **Sustainable Materials**: By integrating recycled materials into their manufacturing processes, Saint-Gobain reduces waste and minimizes environmental impact.

#### Urbanization and Sustainable Construction
Urbanization poses unique challenges, particularly in developing countries where rapid population growth necessitates efficient and sustainable construction practices:

- **Light Construction**: Saint-Gobain focuses on light construction techniques that require fewer resources and have a lower environmental footprint.

- **Global Sustainability Initiatives**: The company launches global sustainability campaigns aimed at promoting environmentally friendly construction practices worldwide.

#### Regulatory and Community Support
Saint-Gobain also engages with regulatory frameworks and community stakeholders to navigate complex environmental challenges:

- **Regulatory Compliance**: Recognizing the varying regulatory environments across different regions, Saint-Gobain adheres to and influences regulations such as taxonomy in Europe and differing systems in the US.

- **Community Engagement**: The company strives to gain broader community support for its sustainability initiatives, recognizing that financial and public sentiment can be crucial factors in the adoption of sustainable practices.

### Conclusion
Saint-Gobain addresses environmental challenges in the built environment through a comprehensive approach that includes reducing carbon emissions, enhancing energy efficiency, preserving natural resources, and promoting sustainable urban development. By integrating innovative technologies and sustainable practices, Saint-Gobain aims to create a more environmentally conscious and resilient built environment.
