Question: Explain the connection between Saint-Gobain's reorganization and its commitment to sustainability.

### Connection Between Saint-Gobain's Reorganization and Commitment to Sustainability

#### Background on Reorganization
Saint-Gobain underwent a significant reorganization to better serve its customers and adapt to market demands. Prior to the reorganization, the company was structured around product lines. However, this approach was deemed insufficient for providing comprehensive solutions to customers. Recognizing this, Saint-Gobain shifted its organizational structure to focus on country-based divisions, enabling a more cohesive and integrated approach to meet customer needs. This change was initiated before the onset of the pandemic, demonstrating the company's proactive stance towards adapting to evolving market conditions (source).

#### Commitment to Sustainability
Saint-Gobain's commitment to sustainability is multifaceted and deeply embedded in its strategic planning and operational practices. The company has made substantial strides in reducing its environmental footprint, particularly in terms of CO2 emissions. Key initiatives include:

- **CO2 Reduction:** Saint-Gobain has managed to decrease its total emissions of CO2 by 27% over a five-year period (source).
- **Energy Efficiency:** The company has grown its turnover while simultaneously lowering its carbon intensity by 42%, showcasing a balance between economic growth and environmental responsibility (source).
- **Internal Carbon Pricing:** Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has implemented an internal carbon price to incentivize more sustainable practices and reduce its overall carbon footprint (source).
- **Product Innovation:** The company has introduced innovative products like low-carbon glass, which reduces CO2 emissions significantly. Additionally, they have started a cluster board plant in Norway that operates with zero carbon emissions due to the use of hydropower electricity (source).

#### Synergies Between Reorganization and Sustainability Efforts
The reorganization and the commitment to sustainability are intrinsically linked through several key factors:

1. **Customer-Centric Solutions:** By restructuring the company into country-based divisions, Saint-Gobain can tailor its offerings to local markets more effectively. This allows for the provision of sustainable solutions that address specific regional needs, such as building materials that enhance energy efficiency and reduce environmental impact (source).
   
2. **Strategic Alignment:** The reorganization facilitates better alignment between different departments and geographical units. This ensures that sustainability initiatives are consistently prioritized across the entire organization, promoting a unified approach to environmental responsibility (source).

3. **Innovation and Collaboration:** The new organizational structure encourages collaboration and innovation, essential components for developing and implementing sustainable technologies and practices. For instance, the company's efforts in low-carbon glass production and energy-efficient building solutions exemplify how strategic reorganization supports environmental goals (source).

4. **Global Impact:** With a country-centric approach, Saint-Gobain can leverage its global network to drive sustainability initiatives worldwide. This includes sharing best practices, collaborating on research and development, and scaling successful projects across different regions (source).

In summary, Saint-Gobain's reorganization has created a robust foundation for advancing its sustainability agenda. By aligning its structure with customer needs and fostering a culture of innovation and collaboration, the company is well-positioned to deliver sustainable solutions that benefit both its stakeholders and the environment.
