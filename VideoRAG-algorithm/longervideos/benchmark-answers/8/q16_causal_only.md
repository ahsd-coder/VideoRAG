### Saint-Gobain's Approach to Engaging and Aligning Its Workforce Around Sustainability Goals

#### Leadership and Vision
Saint-Gobain, under the leadership of its Chief Executive Officers such as Benoît Bazin and others, emphasizes a clear vision and purpose that aligns with sustainability. This vision is centered around light and sustainable construction, which encompasses a range of initiatives aimed at reducing environmental impacts and promoting sustainable practices. The company's commitment is reflected in its strategic plan, referred to as "grow and impact," which focuses on reducing carbon emissions, improving energy efficiency, and preserving natural resources.

#### Diversity and Inclusion
Saint-Gobain recognizes the value of a diverse workforce in driving innovation and sustainability. The company's executive committee comprises individuals from eight different nationalities, highlighting its commitment to inclusivity and cultural diversity. This diversity brings a rich array of backgrounds, cultures, and perspectives that enrich the company's approach to problem-solving and sustainability initiatives.

#### Employee Engagement
The company actively engages its workforce in sustainability efforts. With 168,000 employees globally, Saint-Gobain ensures that every team member is aligned towards common sustainability goals. Employees are encouraged to participate in various initiatives and are provided with the tools and resources necessary to contribute effectively. For instance, the company has implemented internal carbon pricing since 2016, which helps align financial decisions with environmental objectives.

#### Corporate Culture
Saint-Gobain fosters a corporate culture that prioritizes sustainability and environmental stewardship. This culture is evident in the company's approach to innovation, such as the world premiere of running hydrogen in a glass furnace, which demonstrates a commitment to exploring new technologies that minimize environmental impact. The company also emphasizes the importance of aligning different stakeholders, including finance professionals, engineers, and researchers, to ensure that sustainability is integrated into all aspects of the business.

#### Community and Customer Impact
Saint-Gobain's sustainability efforts extend beyond the company's operations to positively impact communities and customers. For example, the company's involvement in retrofitting buildings and providing sustainable solutions for homeowners reflects a broader commitment to creating a better built environment. By offering products that enhance energy efficiency and reduce waste, Saint-Gobain aims to make a significant difference in the lives of its customers while contributing to global environmental goals.

In summary, Saint-Gobain's approach to engaging and aligning its diverse workforce around sustainability goals involves a combination of clear leadership, inclusive culture, active employee participation, and a focus on community and customer impact. Through these efforts, the company strives to lead the way in sustainable construction and environmental responsibility.
