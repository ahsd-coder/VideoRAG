Question: How does Saint-Gobain address the financial barriers homeowners face when considering energy-efficient renovations?

### Addressing Financial Barriers for Energy-Efficient Renovations

Saint-Gobain, a leading company in sustainable building solutions, recognizes the financial hurdles homeowners face when considering energy-efficient renovations. Here are some key strategies and initiatives the company employs to mitigate these barriers:

1. **Subsidies and Public Support**:
   - Saint-Gobain advocates for government subsidies and public support to assist low-income households with the costs of energy-efficient renovations. They propose a "Marshall Plan" to fund such initiatives in France, emphasizing the importance of directing subsidies towards low-income populations to make renovations more accessible.

2. **Regulatory Frameworks**:
   - The company supports the implementation of clear regulatory frameworks that encourage energy efficiency. By providing transparency and guidance, homeowners are better equipped to navigate the process of making their homes more energy-efficient. France, for instance, mandates energy performance diagnostics for transactions, helping to identify necessary improvements and their associated costs.

3. **Internal Carbon Pricing**:
   - Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has implemented an internal carbon price to incentivize sustainable practices internally. This approach can influence broader market dynamics and encourage similar practices among stakeholders, indirectly benefiting homeowners by fostering a more environmentally conscious market.

4. **Innovative Financing Models**:
   - Saint-Gobain promotes innovative financing models that can help homeowners manage the upfront costs of energy-efficient renovations. These models might include energy performance contracting, where the savings from reduced energy bills are used to repay the cost of the renovations over time.

5. **Educational Campaigns**:
   - Through educational campaigns and presentations, Saint-Gobain informs homeowners about the long-term financial benefits of energy-efficient renovations. By highlighting the potential for increased real estate values and reduced energy bills, they aim to shift perceptions and encourage proactive investment in home upgrades.

6. **Collaboration with Local Governments and NGOs**:
   - Saint-Gobain collaborates with local governments and non-governmental organizations to develop community-based programs that support homeowners financially. These partnerships can leverage collective resources to make energy-efficient renovations more affordable and accessible.

By adopting these strategies, Saint-Gobain aims to create a more supportive environment for homeowners looking to improve the energy efficiency of their homes, thereby reducing financial barriers and promoting sustainable living.
