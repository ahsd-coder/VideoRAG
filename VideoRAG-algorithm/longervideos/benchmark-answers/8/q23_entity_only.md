Question: Describe the two essential elements Rajesh Jain believes are necessary for building a strong company culture.

### Essential Elements for Building Strong Company Culture According to Rajesh Jain

Rajesh Jain, the founder of Netcore Cloud, emphasizes two critical elements for building a robust company culture:

1. **Icon**:
   - **Role**: The icon serves as a living embodiment of the company's values and culture.
   - **Importance**: The icon is crucial because they represent the culture of the company, making employees feel connected and inspired to stay committed to the organization.
   - **Example**: In Netcore Cloud, the current CEO, Kalpeth, is referred to as the icon, symbolizing the company’s culture and values.

2. **Storyteller**:
   - **Role**: The storyteller is responsible for conveying the history and essence of the company to new hires and existing employees.
   - **Importance**: Storytelling helps in fostering a sense of belonging and continuity, ensuring that the company’s culture and values are consistently communicated and reinforced.
   - **Example**: Rajesh Jain’s wife, Bhavna, acts as the storyteller at Netcore Cloud, sharing the company’s journey and ethos with new batches of employees.

These elements together help in creating a cohesive and enduring company culture that resonates with employees and contributes to the organization's success.
