Question: How does Saint-Gobain measure the impact of its sustainability initiatives?

### How Saint-Gobain Measures the Impact of Its Sustainability Initiatives

Saint-Gobain employs a multifaceted approach to measure the impact of its sustainability initiatives. Here are some key ways the company evaluates its efforts:

1. **Energy Efficiency and Emissions Reduction**:
   - **Metrics**: Saint-Gobain tracks metrics such as the reduction in carbon emissions, particularly focusing on Scope 1 and 2 emissions. For instance, the company has reported a 27% drop in total CO2 emissions over five years.
   - **Projects**: Initiatives like launching low-carbon glass products and utilizing biogas to reduce CO2 emissions by 40% are quantified to assess their impact.

2. **Resource Conservation**:
   - **Recycling Programs**: The company implements recycling programs where materials are reused in their production processes, thereby conserving natural resources.
   - **Internal Carbon Pricing**: Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has imposed an internal carbon price to encourage departments to adopt more sustainable practices and reduce their carbon footprint.

3. **Acoustic Quality and Educational Environments**:
   - **Impact Studies**: Saint-Gobain conducts studies to measure the impact of their building solutions on educational environments, such as the correlation between classroom acoustics and teacher absenteeism.
   - **School Projects**: By improving the acoustic quality of classrooms, Saint-Gobain aims to enhance learning conditions, thereby contributing to better educational outcomes.

4. **Customer and Team Benefits**:
   - **Customer Satisfaction**: The company measures the benefits of its sustainable practices on customer satisfaction, recognizing that energy-efficient and eco-friendly solutions can lead to higher customer loyalty.
   - **Team Performance**: Saint-Gobain emphasizes that improving energy consumption leads to better performance among teams, aligning sustainability with operational excellence.

5. **Regulatory Compliance and Documentation**:
   - **Before and After Scenarios**: Saint-Gobain documents the environmental impact before and after implementing its solutions to provide evidence of their effectiveness.
   - **Public Bodies Engagement**: The company works to convince public bodies and regulators of the benefits of its solutions by providing measurable data on energy efficiency, acoustic quality, and other factors.

6. **Innovative Solutions**:
   - **World Premiere Technologies**: Saint-Gobain has achieved milestones like running hydrogen in glass furnaces, showcasing innovative approaches to reduce environmental impact.
   - **Cluster Board Plants**: The company operates cluster board plants with zero carbon production, leveraging hydropower electricity to minimize carbon emissions.

By adopting these measurement and evaluation techniques, Saint-Gobain ensures that its sustainability initiatives are not only impactful but also transparent and verifiable, contributing to both environmental and business success.
