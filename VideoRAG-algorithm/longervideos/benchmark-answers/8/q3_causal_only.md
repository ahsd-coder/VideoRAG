Question: According to Rajesh Jain, what is the most common question entrepreneurs face?

According to Rajesh Jain, one of the most common questions entrepreneurs face is whether they should bring in a professional CEO to scale their business. This question often arises during the growth phase of a company, especially when transitioning from a founder-led model to a more professionally managed structure.

In one of the retrieved video clips, Rajesh Jain discusses this topic during a presentation at Columbia Engineering. He mentions that after running the business himself for several years, he replaced himself with a professional CEO in 2008 to facilitate the growth journey of his company, Netcore. He notes that many entrepreneurs face similar dilemmas about whether to retain control or seek external expertise to help scale the business.

Additionally, Jain emphasizes that this decision is not straightforward and depends on various factors, such as the nature of the business and the specific needs of the company at different stages of its development. For instance, some businesses may require upfront investment in infrastructure like warehouses, which necessitates bringing in a CEO with relevant experience.
