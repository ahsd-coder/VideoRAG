Question: What specific low-carbon initiatives has Saint-Gobain implemented?

### Low-Carbon Initiatives Implemented by Saint-Gobain

#### 1. **Low-Carbon Glass Production**
- **Zero Carbon Flat Glass Manufacturing:** Saint-Gobain has achieved a run of zero carbon production of flat glass, marking a significant milestone in reducing CO2 emissions.
- **Low-Carbon Glass Offer:** The company has launched a low-carbon glass product that reduces CO2 emissions by 40%. This initiative utilizes more recycled glass and biogas, along with modifications in the manufacturing process.

#### 2. **Cluster Board Plant with Zero Carbon Production**
- **World's First Cluster Board Plant in Norway:** Saint-Gobain inaugurated the world's first cluster board plant in Norway, achieving zero carbon production thanks to the abundant hydropower electricity available in the region.
- **Future Plans:** The company is planning to establish a similar plant in Quebec, Canada, leveraging hydropower to produce low-carbon woolboard.

#### 3. **Internal Carbon Tax**
- **Capital Expenditures and R&D Projects:** Saint-Gobain has introduced an internal carbon tax of €100 for capital expenditures and €200 for Research and Development (R&D) projects. This measure anticipates the continued increase in carbon prices, encouraging internal efforts to reduce carbon footprints.

#### 4. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**
- **Focus on Existing Buildings:** Recognizing that 85% to 90% of buildings that will be present in 2050 already exist today, Saint-Gobain emphasizes the importance of retrofitting these structures to improve energy efficiency and reduce CO2 emissions.
- **Innovative Materials:** The company has developed glass wool insulation products that are 70% recycled materials and a plasterboard with 50% recycled content, contributing to a circular economy and minimizing environmental impact.

#### 5. **Strategic Initiatives**
- **Sustainable Infrastructure Goals:** Saint-Gobain aims to drop 27% of the total emissions of CO2 (scope 1 and 2) within the group over five years, while simultaneously growing the total turnover and reducing carbon intensity by 42% per euro of turnover.
- **Global Leadership in Light and Sustainable Construction:** The company's strategic plan focuses on worldwide leadership in light and sustainable construction, emphasizing the measurement of the impact of their solutions and the scaling up of these solutions based on customer demand.

These initiatives collectively demonstrate Saint-Gobain's commitment to reducing its carbon footprint and promoting sustainable practices across various sectors of its operations.
