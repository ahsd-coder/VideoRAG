Question: What are the two things that Rajesh Jain believes are required to build a strong company culture?

### Building a Strong Company Culture According to Rajesh Jain

Rajesh Jain, the founder of Netcore Cloud, emphasizes two critical components for building a strong company culture:

1. **An Icon**: 
   - This refers to a person within the organization who embodies the values and culture of the company. 
   - For example, in NetCore, Rajesh Jain himself is described as the "icon," symbolizing honesty, transparency, and a human environment.

2. **A Storyteller**:
   - This individual is responsible for conveying the company’s history, values, and culture to new employees and stakeholders.
   - In NetCore, Rajesh Jain mentions that Bhavna, his wife and the HR head, serves as the storyteller, sharing the essence of what makes NetCore great with each new batch of recruits.

These two roles work together to ensure that the company's culture is not only lived but also communicated effectively, fostering a cohesive and aligned team environment.
