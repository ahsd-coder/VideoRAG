Question: What are the main research and development priorities for Saint-Gobain?

### Main Research and Development Priorities for Saint-Gobain

#### Decarbonization of Processes
One of the primary priorities for Saint-Gobain is decarbonizing their manufacturing processes. This involves reducing carbon emissions across various stages of production, from raw material extraction to product manufacturing. The company is committed to finding innovative ways to decrease its environmental footprint and achieve carbon neutrality.

#### Sustainable Construction Materials
Saint-Gobain focuses on developing sustainable construction materials that promote energy efficiency and environmental conservation. This includes advancements in insulation products, such as glass wool and plasterboards made from recycled materials, which significantly reduce CO2 content and minimize the environmental impact.

#### Circular Economy Practices
Another key priority is implementing circular economy principles within their operations. This involves recycling materials back into their production processes and creating products that can be easily reused or recycled at the end of their lifecycle. For instance, Saint-Gobain has launched glass wool insulation products that are 70% recycled and plasterboards with 50% recycled materials.

#### Light Construction Solutions
The company is also dedicated to providing solutions for light construction. This involves developing materials and systems that enable the creation of lightweight yet strong structures, contributing to sustainable urban development and improved living conditions in emerging markets.

#### Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting
Saint-Gobain emphasizes enhancing energy efficiency in buildings, particularly through retrofitting existing structures. They aim to improve thermal efficiency, reduce energy bills, and enhance the overall sustainability of buildings by integrating advanced insulation and other energy-saving technologies.

#### Innovation and Digital Transformation
Innovation is a cornerstone of Saint-Gobain's R&D efforts. They are investing in digital technologies to streamline operations, improve product development, and enhance customer experiences. This includes leveraging big data, artificial intelligence, and other digital tools to optimize production processes and supply chain management.

#### Environmental Sustainability Initiatives
Saint-Gobain is committed to environmental sustainability through various initiatives. They have implemented internal carbon pricing mechanisms and are exploring alternative energy sources, such as running glass furnaces with hydrogen to reduce environmental impacts. Additionally, they are involved in initiatives to minimize their carbon footprint and contribute to global environmental goals.

These priorities reflect Saint-Gobain's dedication to sustainable development, innovation, and environmental stewardship, positioning the company as a leader in the construction and materials industry.
