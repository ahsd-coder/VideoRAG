Question: How does Saint-Gobain measure the impact of its sustainability initiatives?

### Measuring the Impact of Saint-Gobain's Sustainability Initiatives

#### 1. **Internal Carbon Pricing**
   - Saint-Gobain imposes an internal carbon price since 2016, which helps in assessing and mitigating the environmental impact of their operations. This internal pricing mechanism incentivizes the company to reduce carbon emissions and improve sustainability.

#### 2. **Reduction in Emissions**
   - The company reports a significant drop in total emissions of CO₂, specifically a 27% reduction in scope 1 and 2 emissions over a period of five years. Additionally, the carbon intensity per euro of turnover has decreased by 42%, indicating that the company has managed to reduce emissions while growing its turnover.

#### 3. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofit Projects**
   - Saint-Gobain focuses on improving energy efficiency and retrofitting existing buildings. They recognize that 85-90% of buildings in existence today will still be standing in 2050, thus emphasizing the importance of retrofitting these structures to enhance energy efficiency and reduce carbon footprints.

#### 4. **Product Innovations**
   - The company has launched several innovative products designed to promote sustainability. For instance, they introduced low-carbon glass and initiated the first worldwide cluster board plant in Norway with zero carbon production due to access to hydropower electricity.

#### 5. **Circular Economy and Recycling**
   - Saint-Gobain is committed to the circular economy by recycling materials into their processes. They emphasize the importance of recycling and reusing materials to minimize waste and reduce environmental impact.

#### 6. **Measurement of Impact**
   - The company measures the impact of their solutions, such as the reduction in energy bills and the benefits to customers and employees. By quantifying these impacts, Saint-Gobain ensures that their sustainability initiatives are not only effective but also scalable and beneficial for stakeholders.

#### 7. **Collaboration and Community Engagement**
   - Saint-Gobain collaborates with various stakeholders, including architects and community leaders, to ensure that their sustainability initiatives align with broader societal goals. This includes hiring an architect at the board level to challenge and guide the company’s sustainability practices.

#### 8. **Educational and Research Partnerships**
   - Through partnerships with institutions like Columbia University, Saint-Gobain engages in educational and research collaborations that help drive forward sustainable practices and innovation in construction and urban development.

By implementing these measures, Saint-Gobain demonstrates a holistic approach to sustainability, integrating environmental responsibility into their core business strategy and operations.
