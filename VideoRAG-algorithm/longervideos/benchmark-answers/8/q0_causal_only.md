### Main Research and Development Priorities for Saint-Gobain

Saint-Gobain, a leading company in the construction and materials industry, has identified several key priorities for its research and development (R&D) efforts. These priorities are aimed at addressing environmental challenges and fostering innovation within the company. Here are the main R&D priorities highlighted in the presentations:

1. **Decarbonizing Processes**:
   - Saint-Gobain is committed to reducing carbon emissions across its operations. This includes exploring new technologies and methodologies to decrease the carbon footprint of their manufacturing processes. For example, they have launched low-carbon glass products and are experimenting with hydrogen fuel in glass furnaces to reduce environmental impact.

2. **Systems for Light Construction**:
   - The company is focusing on developing innovative solutions for lightweight construction, which can improve energy efficiency and reduce material usage. This includes advancements in building materials that offer superior thermal insulation properties, thereby lowering energy consumption in buildings.

3. **Circular Economy Initiatives**:
   - Saint-Gobain is actively working on implementing circular economy principles. This involves recycling materials into production processes and creating products with high percentages of recycled content. For instance, they have introduced glass wool insulation products containing 70% recycled materials and plasterboards with 50% recycled content.

4. **New Applications for Materials**:
   - Research is underway to discover new applications for existing materials and develop novel materials that can contribute to sustainable building practices. This includes finding ways to enhance the acoustic performance of classrooms and integrating sustainable practices into architectural designs.

#### Examples and Initiatives

- **Low-Carbon Glass**: Saint-Gobain has launched low-carbon glass products that significantly reduce CO2 emissions during production.
- **Hydrogen Fuel in Glass Furnaces**: The company conducted a world premiere in Germany to run hydrogen in a glass furnace, demonstrating the feasibility of using cleaner fuels in industrial processes.
- **Recycled Materials**: Products like glass wool insulation and plasterboards with high recycled content are being developed to minimize waste and promote sustainability.
- **Energy Efficiency**: Efforts are being made to improve the energy efficiency of buildings through better insulation and innovative construction techniques.

#### Strategic Focus

The company's R&D strategy is aligned with its broader mission of making the world a better place by providing sustainable solutions. This involves not only developing new products but also implementing best practices across all operations to ensure minimal environmental impact. Saint-Gobain's approach is characterized by a holistic view that integrates sustainability into every aspect of its business.

### Conclusion

Saint-Gobain's R&D priorities reflect a commitment to innovation and sustainability. By focusing on decarbonization, light construction, circular economy initiatives, and new material applications, the company aims to lead the way in creating more sustainable and environmentally friendly construction solutions.
