### Explanation of the Proposed Marshall Plan for Building Retrofits

During his presentation at Columbia University, Benoit Bazin, the Chief Executive Officer of Compagnie de Saint-Gobain, proposed a version of the Marshall Plan to address the challenges faced by the building retrofit sector in France. The original Marshall Plan, enacted in the aftermath of World War II, was designed to aid Western European countries in rebuilding their economies and infrastructure. Similarly, Bazin's proposal aims to stimulate and support the retrofitting of buildings to enhance energy efficiency and sustainability.

#### Key Points of Bazin's Proposal:

1. **Public Support and Subsidies**:
   - Bazin advocates for substantial public support through subsidies to facilitate building retrofits. This support is crucial because retrofitting can be expensive, and low-income individuals may struggle to bear the upfront costs.
   
2. **Targeted Assistance for Low-Income Populations**:
   - The plan specifically directs support towards low-income individuals (referred to as FNG in France). This ensures that those most in need receive assistance, making the transition to more energy-efficient homes more accessible.
   
3. **Reducing Out-of-Pocket Expenses**:
   - To encourage participation, Bazin emphasizes the importance of minimizing out-of-pocket expenses for low-income individuals. For instance, if the cost to the individual is reduced to 20% of the total expense, they are more likely to undertake the retrofitting process compared to a scenario where they would need to pay 90%.

#### Intended Impact:

1. **Environmental Benefits**:
   - By retrofitting buildings, the plan aims to significantly reduce energy consumption and CO2 emissions. This is crucial for meeting sustainability goals and combating climate change.
   
2. **Economic Stimulation**:
   - The proposal seeks to stimulate economic activity through increased investment in retrofit projects, thereby creating jobs and boosting local economies.
   
3. **Social Equity**:
   - Ensuring that low-income populations benefit from the program helps to alleviate energy poverty and improves living conditions for those who are most vulnerable.

#### Contextual References:

- **Regulatory Framework**: Bazin references existing French regulations that will make buildings with poor energy performance (rated 'G') unrentable by 2025. This underscores the urgency and necessity of retrofitting older buildings to meet higher energy performance standards.
  
- **Historical Examples**: Bazin draws parallels to historical actions, such as the prohibition of lead paint and asbestos in rental properties, highlighting the importance of proactive regulatory measures for public health and safety.

In summary, Benoit Bazin's proposed Marshall Plan for building retrofits in France aims to leverage public support and subsidies to make energy-efficient upgrades more accessible and affordable, particularly for low-income households. This initiative is expected to yield environmental, economic, and social benefits by promoting widespread adoption of sustainable building practices.
