Question: Explain the concept of 'Tech' as a leadership principle at Saint-Gobain and provide an example of its implementation.

### Concept of 'Tech' as a Leadership Principle at Saint-Gobain

At Saint-Gobain, 'Tech' is a leadership principle that emphasizes trust, empowerment, and collaboration within the organization. This principle underscores the importance of creating a supportive and aligned team environment where employees feel trusted to make decisions and are empowered to innovate. According to Benoit Bazin, the CEO of Saint-Gobain, this approach fosters a positive and supportive team dynamic, enabling the organization to harness the strengths and contributions of all generations within the company.

#### Key Elements of 'Tech':
- **Trust**: Employees are trusted to take on responsibilities and make decisions that align with the company's goals.
- **Empowerment**: Employees are given the freedom and resources to innovate and implement new ideas.
- **Collaboration**: Encourages teamwork and collective decision-making to leverage diverse perspectives and expertise.

### Example of Implementation: HYPERFRAME Project

One notable example of 'Tech' in action is the HYPERFRAME project, which demonstrates Saint-Gobain's commitment to technological innovation and collaboration. HYPERFRAME is a mixed reality wall framing solution designed to enhance productivity and reduce waste in construction projects. Here’s how the principle of 'Tech' was applied:

- **Trust and Empowerment**: The development of HYPERFRAME involved empowering teams to explore new technologies and methodologies, trusting them to deliver innovative solutions.
- **Collaboration**: The project brought together various departments and stakeholders, including engineers, designers, and construction professionals, to collaborate on the design and implementation of the solution.

Through the implementation of HYPERFRAME, Saint-Gobain showcased its dedication to integrating cutting-edge technology with sustainable construction practices, thereby embodying the principles of 'Tech'.

By fostering a culture of trust, empowerment, and collaboration, Saint-Gobain aims to drive innovation and achieve sustainable development goals.
