Question: Explain the concept of 'Tech' as a leadership principle at Saint-Gobain and provide an example of its implementation.

### Concept of 'Tech' at Saint-Gobain

At Saint-Gobain, 'Tech' is a leadership principle that embodies the core values of **trust**, **empowerment**, and **collaboration**. This concept is rooted in fostering an organizational culture where employees feel supported and trusted to perform their roles effectively, thereby enhancing overall productivity and innovation.

#### Trust
Trust is fundamental in 'Tech'. Leaders at Saint-Gobain believe in trusting their employees to make informed decisions and take initiative, which encourages a sense of ownership and accountability. This trust extends to cross-cultural and multinational settings, as evident from the CEO's discussions about managing a diverse team with members from different countries, each with unique cultural backgrounds and governmental demands.

#### Empowerment
Empowerment is another cornerstone of 'Tech'. By empowering employees, Saint-Gobain ensures that every individual feels valued and capable of contributing meaningfully to the organization. This empowerment is not limited to traditional hierarchical structures but extends to promoting internal mobility, allowing employees to develop their skills and expertise across various departments and geographic locations.

#### Collaboration
Collaboration within 'Tech' emphasizes teamwork and collective problem-solving. It encourages employees to work together across different functions and regions to achieve common goals. This collaborative approach is crucial in a multinational company like Saint-Gobain, where effective communication and coordination are essential to navigate the complexities of global business environments.

### Example of Implementation: Internal Mobility Program

One notable example of the implementation of 'Tech' at Saint-Gobain is the **internal mobility program**. This initiative facilitates the movement of employees between different countries and departments, allowing them to gain diverse experiences and perspectives. 

For instance, a manager from Poland might initially be tasked with overseeing operations in their home country. Through the internal mobility program, this manager could later be transferred to a leadership role in another country, such as Germany, where they would be expected to apply their managerial skills and adapt to a new cultural and operational context. This transfer would not only enrich the manager's career trajectory but also contribute to a broader pool of experienced leaders within the company.

By fostering such mobility, Saint-Gobain aims to build a robust network of adaptable and versatile leaders who can effectively manage the company's operations in different parts of the world. This approach underscores the importance of trust, as leaders are given autonomy to succeed in new roles, and empowerment, as employees are encouraged to grow and develop beyond their initial positions. Additionally, collaboration is enhanced as these leaders bring varied experiences and insights to their new roles, facilitating better teamwork and problem-solving across the organization.

In summary, 'Tech' at Saint-Gobain encapsulates a holistic approach to leadership that prioritizes trust, empowerment, and collaboration, exemplified through initiatives like the internal mobility program.
