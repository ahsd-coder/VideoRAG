Question: Describe Saint-Gobain's approach to engaging with and aligning its diverse workforce around sustainability goals.

### Saint-Gobain's Approach to Engaging and Aligning Its Workforce Around Sustainability Goals

#### Leadership and Vision
Saint-Gobain, under the leadership of Benoît Bazin, the Chief Executive Officer, emphasizes a strong commitment to sustainability and environmental responsibility. Bazin's vision for the company revolves around creating more sustainable infrastructure in urban cities, focusing on reducing greenhouse gas emissions and promoting green infrastructure.

#### Diverse Workforce Engagement
Saint-Gobain employs a diverse workforce, with employees from various nationalities contributing to the company's executive committee. This diversity brings a rich array of backgrounds, cultures, and perspectives, which enhances the company's ability to innovate and implement sustainable solutions globally. 

#### Organizational Alignment
The company has successfully aligned its 168,000 employees towards a common goal of sustainability. This alignment is driven by a clear purpose and vision that focuses on light and sustainable construction. The strategic plan, termed "Grow and Impact," aims to integrate sustainability into every aspect of the business, ensuring that growth and environmental impact are mutually supportive.

#### Internal Carbon Pricing and Energy Efficiency
Saint-Gobain has implemented internal carbon pricing since 2016, which helps in minimizing the company's environmental footprint. Additionally, the company has made significant strides in reducing CO2 emissions and improving energy efficiency. For instance, the company has achieved a 27% drop in total emissions of CO2 for scope 1 and 2 within the group over five years, and a 42% reduction in carbon intensity per euro of turnover.

#### Focus on Circular Economy and Recycling
Saint-Gobain places a strong emphasis on the circular economy, particularly in recycling materials into their processes. An example is their initiative to take back all glass used in renovations for reuse, demonstrating a commitment to sustainability that goes beyond traditional practices.

#### Community Support and Collaboration
The company fosters a supportive team environment where employees feel aligned and motivated to contribute to sustainability goals. Bazin highlights the positive energy and support from the team, noting that the alignment and energy derived from all generations worldwide have been extremely positive.

#### Global Impact
Saint-Gobain's approach extends beyond its immediate operations to positively impact customers and the planet. By providing innovative solutions for better-built environments, the company aims to make a tangible difference in the fight against climate change.

### Conclusion
Saint-Gobain's approach to engaging and aligning its diverse workforce around sustainability goals is multifaceted and comprehensive. Through strong leadership, clear vision, and strategic initiatives, the company ensures that sustainability is integrated into all aspects of its operations, fostering a culture of environmental responsibility and innovation.
