Question: How does Saint-Gobain address CO2 emissions in building materials?

### Addressing CO2 Emissions in Building Materials: Saint-Gobain's Approach

#### Introduction
Saint-Gobain, a prominent construction materials company, is actively working to reduce CO2 emissions associated with building materials. Their efforts are multifaceted, encompassing innovative product development, sustainable manufacturing processes, and strategic partnerships.

#### Innovative Product Development
Saint-Gobain has introduced several low-carbon and energy-efficient products to reduce CO2 emissions. For example, they launched the first low-carbon glass, which significantly reduces CO2 emissions during production. Additionally, they have developed light construction techniques that move away from traditional brick and concrete walls to lighter structures like timber frames or steel pillars, thereby reducing the overall weight and embodied CO2 of building materials.

#### Sustainable Manufacturing Processes
The company employs sustainable manufacturing practices to minimize its environmental footprint. They have implemented internal carbon pricing since 2016, which serves as a tool to reduce emissions. By running hydrogen in glass furnaces and using more recycled glass and biogas, Saint-Gobain has managed to launch a low-carbon glass offer with a reduced CO2 footprint. Furthermore, they have started cluster board plants in Norway and Canada, utilizing hydropower electricity to achieve zero carbon production.

#### Strategic Partnerships and Collaborations
Saint-Gobain collaborates with universities and research institutions to advance sustainable construction practices. They leverage these partnerships to develop new technologies and solutions that contribute to reducing CO2 emissions. For instance, they have worked on projects that involve recycling demolition waste and integrating it back into manufacturing processes, demonstrating a commitment to a circular economy approach.

#### Corporate Strategy and Leadership
Under the leadership of executives such as Bertrand Béchain and Benoît Balmus, Saint-Gobain has integrated sustainability into its core strategy. The company aims to balance growth with environmental responsibility by reducing CO2 emissions while expanding its operations. They have set ambitious targets to decrease emissions and improve the carbon intensity of their products.

#### Conclusion
Saint-Gobain addresses CO2 emissions in building materials through a combination of innovative product development, sustainable manufacturing processes, and strategic collaborations. Their comprehensive approach not only seeks to mitigate the environmental impact of construction materials but also sets a precedent for sustainable business practices in the industry.

By adopting these measures, Saint-Gobain demonstrates a commitment to creating a more sustainable built environment and contributing to global efforts to combat climate change.
