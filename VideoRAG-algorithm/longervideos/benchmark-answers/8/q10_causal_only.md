Question: What are Saint-Gobain's main sustainability-focused strategies?

### Saint-Gobain's Main Sustainability-Focused Strategies

#### 1. **Reduction of Greenhouse Gas Emissions**
   - **Carbon Footprint Reduction**: Saint-Gobain aims to significantly reduce its carbon footprint. The company plans to drop CO2 emissions by 27% for scope 1 and 2 within the group over five years. They also aim to achieve a 42% reduction in carbon intensity per euro of turnover.
   - **Internal Carbon Pricing**: Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has imposed an internal carbon price to drive efforts towards minimizing their environmental impact. This internal pricing mechanism is applied globally across 76 countries, ensuring uniformity in addressing carbon emissions.

#### 2. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**
   - **Retrofitting Existing Buildings**: Recognizing that 85%-90% of buildings in 2050 already exist today, Saint-Gobain focuses on retrofitting existing structures to improve energy efficiency. This includes initiatives like acoustic performance improvements in classrooms, which can positively impact teacher absenteeism.
   - **Low-Carbon Products**: The company has launched low-carbon glass products and offers that reduce CO2 emissions. For instance, they introduced a low-carbon glass offer that reduces CO2 emissions by 40% in glass production by utilizing more recycled glass and biogas.

#### 3. **Circular Economy and Resource Conservation**
   - **Recycling and Reuse**: Saint-Gobain implements recycling materials into their processes and promotes a circular economy. They have established worldwide clusters that utilize hydropower electricity for zero-carbon production, such as the first worldwide cluster board plant in Norway.
   - **Resource Efficiency**: The company focuses on maximizing the impact of their solutions while minimizing resource consumption. This includes optimizing the use of raw materials and reducing waste.

#### 4. **Innovative Technologies and Sustainable Construction**
   - **Advanced Manufacturing Processes**: Saint-Gobain employs innovative technologies and sustainable construction methods to minimize environmental impact. For example, they run hydrogen in glass furnaces, which is a pioneering effort to reduce environmental impact.
   - **Collaborative Efforts**: The company collaborates with architects and other stakeholders to integrate sustainable practices into construction projects. This includes using high-performance solutions in modern cities and urban planning.

#### 5. **Leadership and Organizational Change**
   - **Diverse Leadership**: Saint-Gobain emphasizes diversity within its executive committee, comprising eight different nationalities. This diversity brings varied perspectives to address global challenges effectively.
   - **Country-Based Organization**: The company reorganized its structure from product lines to country-based divisions to better serve local markets and implement tailored solutions.

#### 6. **Corporate Responsibility and Community Engagement**
   - **Community Support**: Saint-Gobain prioritizes community support and engagement, fostering a sense of responsibility towards the environment and society. They recognize the importance of being part of the society and making a positive impact.
   - **Academic Partnerships**: The company engages in academic partnerships, such as the Columbia University series, to disseminate knowledge and inspire sustainable practices among the new generation.

These strategies collectively demonstrate Saint-Gobain's commitment to sustainability, focusing on reducing environmental impact, promoting energy efficiency, and fostering innovative solutions in construction and manufacturing.
