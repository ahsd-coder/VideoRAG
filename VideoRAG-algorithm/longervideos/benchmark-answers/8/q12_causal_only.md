### Saint-Gobain's Commitment to Carbon Neutrality

**Overview**
Saint-Gobain, a leading company in the construction and materials industry, has made significant commitments towards achieving carbon neutrality. These commitments are outlined in presentations and discussions featured in various videos and transcripts.

**Key Commitments**

1. **Carbon Neutral Goal by 2050**
   - Saint-Gobain has committed to achieving carbon neutrality by 2050. This long-term goal reflects the company's dedication to addressing environmental challenges and reducing its carbon footprint significantly over the coming decades.

2. **Reduction Targets**
   - The company has already achieved notable reductions in carbon emissions. According to one presentation, Saint-Gobain has reduced total CO2 emissions by 27% for scopes 1 and 2 over five years, while simultaneously increasing total turnover. Additionally, the carbon intensity per euro of turnover has dropped by 42%.

3. **Internal Carbon Pricing**
   - To drive these reductions, Saint-Gobain imposes an internal carbon price on itself. Since 2016, this internal pricing mechanism has been used to incentivize and prioritize investments and projects that align with carbon reduction goals.

4. **Sustainability Initiatives**
   - Various initiatives have been launched to support these targets. For instance, the company has introduced low-carbon glass products and zero-carbon production of flat glass. These innovations demonstrate Saint-Gobain's commitment to developing sustainable materials and processes.

5. **Focus on Building Efficiency**
   - Recognizing the significant contribution of the built environment to global CO2 emissions (approximately 37-40%), Saint-Gobain emphasizes the importance of improving building efficiency and reducing emissions throughout a building's lifecycle. 

6. **Circular Economy Practices**
   - The company is also focused on adopting circular economy principles, such as recycling materials into processes and producing products with high percentages of recycled content. For example, glass wool insulation products are made from 70% recycled materials, and plasterboards have 50% recycled content.

7. **Global Leadership**
   - Saint-Gobain aims to be a worldwide leader in light and sustainable construction, integrating these sustainability goals into their strategic plans and business operations.

**Conclusion**
Saint-Gobain's commitment to carbon neutrality encompasses a comprehensive approach that includes setting ambitious targets, implementing innovative technologies, and fostering sustainable practices across its operations. By prioritizing these initiatives, the company strives to minimize its environmental impact and contribute positively to global efforts in combating climate change.
