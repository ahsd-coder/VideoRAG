Question: What environmental challenges does Saint-Gobain address in the built environment?

### Environmental Challenges Addressed by Saint-Gobain in the Built Environment

#### 1. **Carbon Emissions Reduction**
   - **Built Environment Contribution**: The built environment is responsible for a significant portion of global CO2 emissions, estimated to be around 37-40%. 
   - **Company Initiatives**: Saint-Gobain is committed to reducing its carbon footprint and aims to achieve carbon neutrality. They emphasize the importance of sustainable building practices and innovative solutions to decrease emissions from buildings throughout their lifecycle.

#### 2. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**
   - **Current Building Stock**: A substantial portion of buildings expected to be in existence in 2050 already exist today, with estimates ranging from 85% to 90%.
   - **Retrofit Solutions**: Saint-Gobain focuses on improving energy efficiency in existing buildings through retrofitting. This includes enhancing insulation, upgrading windows, and integrating smart technologies to reduce energy consumption and lower CO2 emissions.

#### 3. **Natural Resource Preservation**
   - **Sustainable Materials**: The company works on developing and utilizing sustainable building materials that require fewer natural resources and have minimal environmental impact.
   - **Recycling and Reuse**: Initiatives include recycling materials into manufacturing processes and launching low-carbon products like low-carbon glass and insulation solutions.

#### 4. **Light Construction Methods**
   - **Alternative Building Techniques**: Moving away from heavy concrete structures towards lighter construction methods can significantly reduce the amount of concrete used, thus lowering emissions.
   - **Steel Frames**: Proposing alternatives such as steel frames for skyscrapers and reducing reliance on full concrete blocks can contribute to more sustainable building practices.

#### 5. **Acoustic Performance and Classroom Design**
   - **Impact on Education**: Saint-Gobain recognizes the importance of acoustic quality in classrooms, linking it to factors such as teacher absenteeism and student performance.
   - **Integrated Solutions**: Providing comprehensive building materials and solutions to enhance acoustic performance in educational facilities is part of their strategy.

#### 6. **Circular Economy**
   - **Material Recycling**: Implementing a circular economy approach where materials are recycled and reused in manufacturing processes reduces waste and conserves resources.
   - **Zero Carbon Production**: Examples include the launch of zero carbon glass production and the establishment of worldwide cluster board plants with zero carbon emissions in locations like Norway, leveraging hydropower electricity.

#### 7. **Global Leadership in Sustainable Construction**
   - **Strategic Focus**: Saint-Gobain aims to lead in light and sustainable construction, emphasizing the growth and impact of their sustainable solutions.
   - **Customer Demand**: Recognizing that customers increasingly seek sustainable options, the company scales up its solutions based on demand, promoting wider adoption of eco-friendly building practices.

#### Conclusion
Saint-Gobain addresses a wide range of environmental challenges in the built environment through a multifaceted approach that includes reducing carbon emissions, enhancing energy efficiency, preserving natural resources, adopting light construction methods, improving acoustic performance, promoting a circular economy, and fostering global leadership in sustainable construction. These efforts reflect a commitment to innovative solutions and sustainable practices that contribute to a healthier planet and better-built environments.
