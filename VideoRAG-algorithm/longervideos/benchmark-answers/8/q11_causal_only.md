### How Did Saint-Gobain Reorganize to Address Sustainability Challenges?

#### Strategic Reorganization and Focus Areas

Saint-Gobain, under the leadership of its Chief Executive Officers, has undergone significant reorganization to tackle sustainability challenges. The company shifted its organizational structure from being product-line oriented to being country-based. This change was implemented to enhance the ability to provide comprehensive solutions tailored to specific regional needs rather than just selling individual products.

#### Central Themes and Challenges

The company identified three main challenges:

1. **CO2 Emissions**: Saint-Gobain recognizes that the built environment contributes significantly to CO2 emissions, making it essential to reduce these emissions through sustainable practices.
   
2. **Natural Resource Preservation**: Ensuring the sustainable use of natural resources is critical, especially in light of the increasing global population and the need for construction in emerging markets.

3. **Population Growth and Urban Development**: With projections of 2 billion new inhabitants in major cities over the next few decades, Saint-Gobain focuses on building sustainable and efficient infrastructure to accommodate this growth.

#### Initiatives and Strategies

To address these challenges, Saint-Gobain has implemented several initiatives:

1. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**: The company emphasizes the importance of improving energy efficiency in existing buildings, which is a significant step towards reducing CO2 emissions. Saint-Gobain works on retrofitting old buildings to enhance their energy performance.

2. **Circular Economy and Recycling**: Saint-Gobain promotes a circular economy by recycling materials into their manufacturing processes. This includes managing demolition waste effectively and integrating recycled materials back into production cycles.

3. **Innovative Solutions and Technology**: The company invests in research and development to create innovative solutions that contribute to sustainability. For example, Saint-Gobain has achieved a world premiere by running hydrogen in a glass furnace, demonstrating progress in sustainable energy usage.

4. **Internal Carbon Pricing**: Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has imposed an internal carbon price across its operations in 76 countries, regardless of the existence of external carbon taxes. This approach ensures that sustainability is considered in all business decisions.

5. **Global Leadership in Sustainable Construction**: Saint-Gobain aims to lead the industry in sustainable construction, providing solutions that improve the built environment while reducing environmental impact.

#### Leadership and Stakeholder Engagement

Saint-Gobain's leadership, including its CEO Benoît Bazin, emphasizes the importance of engaging stakeholders and fostering a collaborative environment. The company leverages the diverse backgrounds and expertise of its executives, representing multiple nationalities, to bring innovative ideas and solutions to the table.

#### Conclusion

Through a combination of strategic reorganization, focused initiatives, and innovative solutions, Saint-Gobain is positioning itself as a leader in sustainable construction. By addressing key challenges such as CO2 emissions, resource preservation, and urban development, the company aims to create a more sustainable future.
