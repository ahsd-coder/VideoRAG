Question: What are Saint-Gobain's main sustainability-focused strategies?

### Saint-Gobain's Main Sustainability-Focused Strategies

#### 1. **Circular Economy and Resource Efficiency**
   - **Recycling Materials**: Saint-Gobain is committed to a circular economy by recycling materials into their manufacturing processes. This includes utilizing recycled glass and biogas to reduce carbon emissions and enhance resource efficiency.
   - **Internal Carbon Pricing**: Since 2016, the company has implemented an internal carbon pricing mechanism to ensure that environmental costs are factored into decision-making processes.

#### 2. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**
   - **Building Retrofits**: Recognizing the significant contribution of existing buildings to global CO2 emissions, Saint-Gobain focuses on retrofitting these structures to improve energy efficiency. Their goal is to address the 85-90% of buildings that will remain in use in 2050.
   - **Low-Carbon Products**: The company has launched low-carbon glass and other products designed to reduce energy consumption and CO2 emissions. For instance, they introduced the first-ever low-carbon glass product and a worldwide cluster board plant in Norway powered by hydropower electricity.

#### 3. **Innovation and Product Development**
   - **Sustainable Product Design**: Saint-Gobain develops innovative building materials that enhance the sustainability of construction projects. Examples include products that improve acoustic performance in classrooms and those designed to minimize waste.
   - **Acoustic Solutions**: Research shows a strong correlation between classroom acoustics and teacher absenteeism, highlighting the importance of investing in acoustic quality to improve educational environments.

#### 4. **Leadership and Corporate Responsibility**
   - **Global Leadership**: Saint-Gobain positions itself as a leader in sustainable construction, aiming to influence the industry towards more environmentally responsible practices. They strive to make their solutions scalable and impactful.
   - **Diverse Leadership**: The company’s executive committee comprises individuals from diverse backgrounds, cultures, and nationalities, enriching their approach to global sustainability challenges.

#### 5. **Community Engagement and Collaboration**
   - **Engaging the Next Generation**: Saint-Gobain recognizes the crucial role of the younger generation in driving sustainability efforts. They encourage and support contributions from the new generation to address urgent environmental issues.
   - **Stakeholder Collaboration**: The company collaborates with stakeholders, including universities and policymakers, to foster innovation and implement sustainable solutions effectively.

By integrating these strategies, Saint-Gobain aims to achieve a balance between economic growth and environmental responsibility, positioning itself as a pioneer in sustainable construction practices.
