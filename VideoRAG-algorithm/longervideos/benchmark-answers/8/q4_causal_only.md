Question: What are the two things that Rajesh Jain believes are required to build a strong company culture?

According to Rajesh Jain, building a strong company culture requires two key elements:

1. **An Icon**: This is someone who embodies the organization's values and vision. The icon serves as a living representation of what the company stands for and inspires others within the organization to align with those values.

2. **A Storyteller**: This individual is responsible for communicating the organization’s values and vision effectively to all stakeholders. They help in spreading the culture and ensuring that everyone within the company understands and embraces the core principles that guide the organization.

These elements are crucial for fostering a robust culture that goes beyond financial incentives and helps maintain employee engagement and retention.
