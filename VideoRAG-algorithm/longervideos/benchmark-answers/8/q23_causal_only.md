### Essential Elements for Building a Strong Company Culture According to Rajesh Jain

Based on the retrieved information, Rajesh Jain, during a presentation or lecture, emphasized two crucial elements necessary for building a robust company culture:

1. **An Icon**

   - **Role**: This individual serves as a living representation of the values and ethos that the company strives to uphold.
   - **Example**: Rajesh Jain gave the example of his CEO, Kalpeth, at NetCorps, who acts as the icon for the company. 

2. **A Storyteller**
   - **Definition**: The storyteller is responsible for conveying the history and values of the company to new members.
   - **Role**: This person helps maintain continuity and reinforces the company’s identity and mission.
   - **Example**: In the case of NetCorps, Rajesh Jain mentioned his wife, Bhavna, as the storyteller. She shares the stories of the company with every new batch of employees, helping them understand what makes the company great.
