Question: What is Saint-Gobain's commitment regarding carbon neutrality?

### Saint-Gobain's Commitment to Carbon Neutrality

Saint-Gobain, a leading company in light and sustainable construction, has made significant commitments toward achieving carbon neutrality. Here are some key points from their initiatives:

1. **Long-Term Goals**:
   - **2050 Carbon Neutral Target**: Saint-Gobain has committed to becoming carbon neutral by 2050. This goal reflects a long-term strategic commitment to address climate change and reduce carbon emissions.

2. **Reduction Targets**:
   - **Scope 1 and 2 Emissions**: The company has achieved a 27% reduction in total emissions of CO2 for scope 1 and 2 within the group over a five-year period. Scope 1 covers direct emissions from owned or controlled sources, while scope 2 includes indirect emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling consumed by the reporting entity.
   
   - **Carbon Intensity Reduction**: Saint-Gobain has managed to decrease the carbon intensity per euro of turnover by 42%, demonstrating that growth can occur alongside a reduction in emissions.

3. **Internal Carbon Pricing**:
   - Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has implemented an internal carbon price to incentivize environmentally friendly decision-making within the organization. This practice helps integrate carbon costs into capital expenditure and research and development (R&D) projects, ensuring that sustainability is a core consideration.

4. **Sustainable Construction Solutions**:
   - **Light and Sustainable Construction**: The company aims to lead in light and sustainable construction practices, focusing on innovative solutions that reduce the environmental impact of buildings. This includes the development of low-carbon glass products and other building materials designed to minimize the carbon footprint of constructions.

5. **Circular Economy Practices**:
   - Saint-Gobain is actively involved in circular economy initiatives, which include recycling materials into their processes and producing products with high recycled content. For instance, their glass wool insulation products are made from 70% recycled materials, and they have launched plasterboards with 50% recycled content.

6. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**:
   - Recognizing the significant contribution of existing buildings to carbon emissions, Saint-Gobain emphasizes the importance of energy efficiency and retrofitting. They aim to improve the energy performance of buildings, thereby reducing CO2 emissions over the building's lifetime.

7. **Global Leadership**:
   - Saint-Gobain is committed to being a worldwide leader in sustainable construction, with a focus on providing solutions that contribute to better-built environments across the globe. Their efforts are aligned with the United Nations' goals and involve strategic planning and innovation to achieve long-term sustainability objectives.

In summary, Saint-Gobain's commitment to carbon neutrality is multifaceted, encompassing reductions in direct emissions, adoption of internal carbon pricing mechanisms, and the development of sustainable construction solutions. These efforts are part of a broader strategy to address environmental challenges and promote sustainable practices in the built environment.
