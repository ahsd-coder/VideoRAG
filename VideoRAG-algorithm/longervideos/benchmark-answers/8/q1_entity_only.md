Question: What is Saint-Gobain's strategy for reducing their carbon footprint in the production of flat glass?

### Saint-Gobain's Strategy for Reducing Carbon Footprint in Flat Glass Production

#### 1. **Sustainability Initiatives**
   - **Women-Employment Initiative:** Saint-Gobain has introduced a unique initiative involving 100% women employees at the mirror line, highlighting a focus on diversity and inclusion within their workforce.
   - **Advanced Robotic Technology:** Integration of advanced robotics for precision tasks, such as handling glass panels, ensures efficiency and minimizes human error.
   - **Monitoring Stations:** Implementation of monitoring stations with computer screens allows for ongoing surveillance and control over the manufacturing process, ensuring quality and safety standards are maintained.
   - **Sustainable Practices:** Emphasis on resource conservation, energy conservation, and biodiversity. Installation and maintenance of solar panels help reduce the carbon footprint through renewable energy sources.

#### 2. **Environmental Measures**
   - **Low-Carbon Gas Offers:** Launching low-carbon gas offers that reduce CO2 emissions by 40%. This is achieved by using more recycled glass and biogas, and adjusting process parameters.
   - **Cluster Board Plant in Norway:** Introduction of the first worldwide cluster board plant in Norway with zero carbon production due to abundant hydropower electricity.
   - **Similar Project in Canada:** Plans for a similar project in Quebec, Canada, utilizing hydropower to ensure zero carbon production.

#### 3. **Long-Term Commitments**
   - **Carbon Neutrality by 2050:** Saint-Gobain has committed to becoming carbon neutral by 2050, aligning with the United Nations' goals.
   - **Rebuilding Cycles:** Recognizing that production lines for flat glass last approximately 20 years before needing reconstruction, the company ensures these lines are rebuilt with sustainable practices in mind.
   - **Pilot Projects:** By 2025, the company aims to have pilot projects in place to test and implement new technologies and methods that will help achieve carbon neutrality by 2050.

#### 4. **Research and Development**
   - **Internal Carbon Tax:** Imposing an internal carbon price since 2016 to incentivize sustainable practices across all investments and acquisitions in the 76 countries where Saint-Gobain operates.
   - **Circular Economy Models:** Working on circular economy models to reduce CO2 content in products and minimize environmental footprints. Examples include glass wool insulation products with 70% recycled materials and plasterboard made from 50% recycled materials.

#### 5. **Strategic Planning**
   - **Investment in Research and Development (R&D):** Investing in R&D projects that consider internal carbon taxes, anticipating increasing carbon prices and ensuring all projects contribute positively to reducing carbon emissions.
   - **Focus on Acoustic Quality and Thermal Efficiency:** Measuring the impact of solutions, such as the correlation between classroom acoustic quality and teacher absenteeism, to improve overall building efficiency and comfort.

By implementing these strategies, Saint-Gobain demonstrates a comprehensive approach to reducing their carbon footprint in the production of flat glass, combining innovative technology, sustainable practices, and long-term commitments to environmental stewardship.
