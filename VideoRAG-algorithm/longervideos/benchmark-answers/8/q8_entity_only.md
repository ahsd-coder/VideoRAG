Question: What specific low-carbon initiatives has Saint-Gobain implemented?

### Low-Carbon Initiatives Implemented by Saint-Gobain

#### 1. **Low-Carbon Glass Production**
   - **Launch of Low-Carbon Glass**: Saint-Gobain launched a low-carbon glass product, marking a significant step towards reducing the carbon footprint in glass manufacturing.
   - **Zero Carbon Production**: The company achieved zero carbon production for flat glass, demonstrating a commitment to minimizing environmental impact.

#### 2. **Internal Carbon Pricing**
   - **Implementation of Internal Carbon Tax**: Since 2016, Saint-Gobain has imposed an internal carbon price to incentivize reductions in carbon emissions across its operations.
   - **Scope of Implementation**: This initiative applies globally across the company’s 76 operational countries, ensuring consistent standards regardless of regional carbon tax policies.

#### 3. **Hydrogen Use in Glass Furnaces**
   - **World Premiere**: Saint-Gobain successfully ran hydrogen in a glass furnace in Germany, marking a world-first in this technology.
   - **Environmental Benefits**: Using hydrogen in glass furnaces helps mitigate environmental challenges associated with traditional fuels, such as water contamination and other emissions.

#### 4. **Circular Economy Practices**
   - **Recycling Materials**: The company actively recycles materials back into its manufacturing processes, promoting a circular economy.
   - **Innovative Materials**: Saint-Gobain has developed innovative materials like naturally carbonated substances, such as holastonite, to replace conventional materials like calcium carbonate.

#### 5. **Energy Efficiency and Retrofitting**
   - **Focus on Retrofits**: The company emphasizes the importance of improving energy efficiency in existing buildings, recognizing that a majority of buildings in 2050 will be those currently standing.
   - **Technological Innovations**: Saint-Gobain is involved in developing technologies that enhance energy efficiency, including passive house innovations and advanced insulation materials.

#### 6. **Sustainable Building Materials**
   - **Glass Wool Insulation Products**: Saint-Gobain produces glass wool insulation products that are 70% recycled materials, significantly reducing the carbon footprint of construction materials.
   - **Plasterboard with Recycled Content**: The company has also introduced plasterboard containing 50% recycled materials, further contributing to sustainable building practices.

#### 7. **Plant-Based Biomass Production**
   - **Rice Husk Biomass**: In India, Saint-Gobain utilizes rice husks as a renewable energy source for its gypsum wallboard plants, demonstrating a commitment to utilizing local agricultural waste.
   - **Innovative Solutions**: By converting agricultural waste into usable energy, Saint-Gobain reduces reliance on non-renewable sources and contributes to local economies.

#### 8. **Strategic Partnerships and Collaboration**
   - **Global Sustainability War for Architecture**: Saint-Gobain collaborates with architects and other stakeholders to promote sustainable architecture, integrating sustainability into design and construction processes.
   - **Diverse Leadership**: The company’s executive committee comprises individuals from various nationalities, fostering a diverse approach to sustainability initiatives.

These initiatives collectively demonstrate Saint-Gobain's commitment to reducing its carbon footprint and promoting sustainable practices across its operations and supply chain.
