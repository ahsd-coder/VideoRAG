### Understanding the Application of SCI Standard to AI

The **Software Carbon Intensity (SCI)** standard is a measure developed by organizations such as IBM and others, including Columbia University, aimed at assessing the carbon footprint of software operations. In the context of Artificial Intelligence (AI), the SCI standard seeks to evaluate and reduce the energy consumption per unit of work performed by AI systems.
