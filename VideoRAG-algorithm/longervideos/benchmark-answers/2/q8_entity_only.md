Question: What is the idea behind the air sensors lab at Columbia's Lamont Doherty Earth Observatory?

### Overview of the Air Sensors Lab at Columbia University’s Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory

The **Columbia-LDEO Air Sensors Lab** at Columbia University’s Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory focuses on investigating air quality through various sensor setups, ranging from low-cost to high-grade monitors. The lab employs a multi-faceted approach involving collaborations with multiple organizations and the utilization of advanced monitoring technology.

#### Key Components:

1. **Sensor Types:**
   - The lab features both regulatory/research grade monitors (e.g., GRIMM PM2.5/PM10) alongside low-cost sensor devices from different vendors such as IQAir, PurpleAir, QUANTAO, and others.
   
2. **Research Goals:**
   - The primary goal is to compare data collected by these sensors to understand their accuracy relative to each other. This comparison helps in validating the reliability of lower cost sensors against more expensive models.

3. **Collaborations:**
   - Several collaborations exist with various organizations, including GAPA (Global Alliance on Air Pollution) and others focused on air quality studies.
   
4. **Projects and Applications:**
   - The lab has been involved in significant projects such as "Low-Cost Investigation into Sources of PM2s" in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of Congo, showcasing the diversity and geographical reach of their research initiatives.
   
5. **Field Installations:**
   - Air sensor installations are often deployed on rooftops with tree-covered surroundings to ensure accurate data collection under varied environmental conditions.

#### Notable Highlights:

- The lab actively engages in capacity-building efforts by training individuals involved in these projects, emphasizing the importance of reliable and accessible air quality monitoring.
- Emphasis is placed on developing correction models for low-cost sensors to enhance their accuracy, thereby making them more widely applicable.

Overall, the Air Sensors Lab at Columbia’s Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory aims to contribute significantly towards understanding global air pollution trends and improving data availability in underrepresented areas. Through its collaborative efforts and innovative use of technology, it plays a pivotal role in advancing environmental research and public health initiatives related to air quality monitoring.
