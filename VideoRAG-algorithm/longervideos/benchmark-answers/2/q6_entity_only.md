The **Software Carbon Intensity (SCI)** is a metric designed to measure the carbon footprint associated with software applications and systems. Its application in the context of Artificial Intelligence (AI) aims to assess and reduce the environmental impact of AI computing processes.

### Conclusion

The Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) standard serves as an essential metric for assessing the carbon footprint of AI applications. By focusing on both operational and embodied carbon emissions, SCI helps to identify areas where improvements can be made to enhance the sustainability of AI systems. This includes measuring energy usage per token in large language models, reducing water consumption, and considering material usage during manufacturing processes. The ongoing efforts by industry bodies like the Green Software Foundation are crucial for advancing this standard specifically within the realm of artificial intelligence.
