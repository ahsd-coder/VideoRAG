### Challenges Faced by Mother Bears Securing Salmon

Mother bears face significant challenges when trying to secure salmon, particularly during times of high water levels. Here are some key obstacles they encounter:

1. **Competition with Male Bears:**
   - **Strategic Maneuvering:** As highlighted in the video footage, a mother bear navigates through rocky riverbanks and dense forests to reach prime fishing spots where salmon are most active.
   - **Prime Fishing Territory Occupation:** A large male bear often occupies these optimal areas. The mother must cautiously approach this territory while maintaining her safety, as demonstrated when she moves closer to the big male without immediate confrontation.

2. **Environmental Conditions:**
   - **Higher Water Levels:** With higher water levels due to seasonal changes or increased rainfall, access to traditional fishing spots is restricted.
   - **Cascading Waterfalls and Riverbanks:** Mother bears must navigate treacherous environments like cascading waterfalls and rocky riverbanks to reach salmon-rich areas.

3. **Risk of Aggression:**
   - **Territorial Behavior:** The presence of male bears in prime fishing territories increases the risk of aggressive encounters, as seen when a mother bear cautiously approaches despite the large male's territorial stance.
   - **Maintaining Calm and Caution:** Mother bears need to remain cautious and avoid provoking larger males to prevent potential conflicts or injuries.

4. **Resource Scarcity:**
   - **Limited Access:** High water levels can reduce available fishing spots, making it harder for mother bears to find sufficient food resources.
   - **Competition with Other Animals:** Besides bear competition, other predators such as otters and even smaller mammals may also be vying for the same fish resources.

5. **Protecting Cubs:**
   - **Ensuring Safety:** Mother bears must ensure their cubs' safety while securing enough food. This involves careful navigation through challenging terrains to avoid dangers like deep water or aggressive males.
   - **Balancing Survival Needs:** The mother bear's primary focus is on providing for her cubs, which demands constant vigilance and strategic movement.

### Conclusion
Mother bears face a multitude of challenges when securing salmon. These include navigating dangerous terrains, competing with larger males for prime fishing spots, and managing resource scarcity due to environmental conditions. Their ability to protect their cubs while obtaining necessary food is crucial for survival in these demanding circumstances.
