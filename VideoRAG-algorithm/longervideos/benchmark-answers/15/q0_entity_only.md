Question: How has the role of the Game Awards evolved over the past 10 years, according to Hideo Kojima?

### Evolution of The Game Awards According to Hideo Kojima

According to Hideo Kojima, a renowned figure in the gaming industry who has been associated with The Game Awards (TGA) since its inception in 2014, the role of The Game Awards has significantly evolved over the past decade. Kojima's insights highlight the growing importance and influence of TGA within the gaming community.

#### Early Years (2014-2018)
In the early years, The Game Awards served primarily as a platform to celebrate and recognize the achievements of the gaming industry. It provided a momentous occasion for the industry to come together, acknowledge outstanding work, and generate excitement for upcoming titles. Kojima's participation as a presenter and frequent attendee underscored the event's significance and helped establish it as a credible and respected annual event.

#### Expansion and Global Reach (2018-2022)
By 2018, The Game Awards had expanded its scope and reach globally. Kojima highlighted the increasing number of international and independent game nominations, reflecting the growing diversity and inclusivity of the gaming industry. The event began to celebrate not just AAA titles but also indie games from various regions, such as Japan, China, and Canada. This expansion emphasized the event's role in fostering a more inclusive and collaborative gaming community.

#### Recognition and Innovation (2022-Present)
Over the past few years, The Game Awards has continued to evolve, focusing on recognizing not only game development achievements but also the broader impact of the gaming industry. Kojima noted the importance of game direction and the collaborative efforts of teams, both large and small, in bringing cohesive and visionary projects to life. The event has increasingly become a platform for celebrating the leadership and creativity that drives the industry forward.

Additionally, The Game Awards has taken on a role in addressing industry challenges and advocating for positive change. Kojima highlighted issues such as job losses and the need for skilled professionals, emphasizing the event's responsibility to foster a healthy and sustainable gaming ecosystem.

### Conclusion
Hideo Kojima's observations reflect The Game Awards' transformation from a simple recognition event to a multifaceted celebration that acknowledges diverse achievements, fosters community, and addresses industry-wide concerns. This evolution underscores TGA's growing importance in shaping the future of the gaming industry.
