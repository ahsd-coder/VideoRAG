Question: Beyond technical performance, what personal aspects should traders track in their journals for self-evaluation?

### Personal Aspects Traders Should Track in Journals for Self-Evaluation

Traders often focus on technical performance metrics such as win-loss percentages, equity curves, and trade results. However, it's equally crucial to track personal aspects that can significantly influence trading outcomes. Here are some key areas to consider:

1. **Emotional State**:
   - **Rating Emotions**: Track your emotional state before, during, and after each trade using a scale (e.g., 1-10). For instance, you might rate how anxious or confident you feel.
   - **Mental Stability**: Document any thoughts or feelings that may have affected your trading decisions. This can help identify patterns or triggers that lead to poor decision-making.

2. **Confidence Levels**:
   - **Entry Decisions**: Note the level of confidence you had when entering a trade. Were you hesitant or overly optimistic?
   - **Consistency**: Evaluate whether your confidence levels correlate with the success of your trades. Overconfidence or lack thereof can lead to suboptimal trading behavior.

3. **Decision-Making Process**:
   - **Reasons for Trades**: Record the reasons behind each trade decision. Did you follow your trading plan strictly, or did you deviate due to external factors?
   - **Impulse vs. Strategy**: Differentiate between trades made based on impulse versus those aligned with your trading strategy.

4. **External Influences**:
   - **News Events**: Log any significant news events or market developments that influenced your trading decisions.
   - **Market Conditions**: Assess how different market conditions (bullish, bearish, volatile) affect your trading choices.

5. **Learning and Adaptation**:
   - **Review Sessions**: Schedule regular review sessions to assess your trading performance and identify areas for improvement.
   - **Adjustments**: Document any adjustments you make to your trading plan based on your self-evaluation. Reflect on whether these changes led to better outcomes.

6. **Behavioral Patterns**:
   - **Habits and Routines**: Keep track of any habitual behaviors that may impact your trading, such as pre-market routines or post-trade reflections.
   - **Risk Management**: Evaluate your adherence to risk management principles, including position sizing and stop-loss placement.

By integrating these personal aspects into your trading journal, you can gain deeper insights into your trading behavior and psychological state. This holistic approach can help you refine your trading strategies and improve long-term profitability.
