Question: Why is it considered unwise to solely rely on trading strategies obtained from external sources?

### Why Relying Solely on External Trading Strategies is Unwise

#### Understanding the Risks

Several experts advise against relying entirely on trading strategies derived from external sources due to several critical reasons:

1. **Lack of Personal Adaptation**:
   - **Expert Opinion**: A speaker in one video emphasized that adopting a strategy from another trader and applying it blindly is not sufficient for becoming a successful trader. Successful traders continuously adapt and refine their strategies based on their unique circumstances and market conditions.
   - **Example**: The video mentions that even if two traders follow the same strategy, one might succeed while the other fails due to differences in their approach and market interpretation.

2. **Risk Management Issues**:
   - **Expert Opinion**: Another video highlighted that traders often fail to manage their positions correctly, especially after a winning streak. Over-aggressive increases in position size can lead to larger losses during subsequent downturns.
   - **Example**: The speaker noted that traders might increase their position sizes too aggressively after a series of wins, leading to disproportionate losses during inevitable drawdowns.

3. **Mental and Emotional Preparedness**:
   - **Expert Opinion**: An individual stressed the importance of emotional stability and mental readiness before entering trades. They suggested that traders should only engage in trades when their emotional state is stable, typically rated between 5 and 6 on a scale of 1 to 10.
   - **Example**: The video advised against entering trades when feeling desperate or overly fearful, advocating for a balanced emotional state to ensure rational decision-making.

4. **Inadequate Control and Understanding**:
   - **Expert Opinion**: Several videos warned against taking signals from external sources without fully understanding the underlying strategy and its implementation. Traders must comprehend the probabilities and mental discipline required to stick with a plan.
   - **Example**: A video cautioned against relying on signal services, stating that traders often lack the mental capacity to adhere strictly to such plans and understand the associated risks.

5. **Educational Pitfalls**:
   - **Expert Opinion**: Some videos pointed out that excessive learning from various sources can lead to confusion and a lack of direction. It's recommended to focus on a few reliable mentors or strategies rather than trying to learn everything.
   - **Example**: A speaker advised focusing on a single direction or mentor to avoid overwhelming oneself with conflicting information.

#### Conclusion

Relying solely on external trading strategies without personal adaptation, thorough understanding, and emotional preparation can lead to poor risk management, increased stress, and suboptimal trading performance. Successful traders emphasize the importance of continuous learning, adapting strategies to fit individual strengths and weaknesses, and maintaining emotional stability.
