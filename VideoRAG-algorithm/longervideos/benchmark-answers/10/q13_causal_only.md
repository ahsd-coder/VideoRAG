Question: Why is it considered unwise to solely rely on trading strategies obtained from external sources?

### Why Solely Relying on External Trading Strategies Can Be Unwise

Solely relying on trading strategies obtained from external sources is generally considered unwise for several reasons:

1. **Lack of Personalization**:
   - **Foundational Knowledge**: Successful trading often requires adapting strategies to fit individual trading styles and risk tolerances. According to the video, one must pick a strategy that suits personal preferences, timeframe, and trading habits (T48).
   - **Different Eyes and Personalities**: Even the most profitable strategy can yield different results for different traders due to variations in personality and approach (T48).

2. **Risk of Misinformation**:
   - **Cautious Advice**: Several videos advise against trusting signals, giving money to wolves, and learning from multiple sources without a clear direction (T37, T40, T43).
   - **Exploitation Risks**: Individuals exploiting others' children to click on trading signals and claim profitability are criticized as criminals (T43).

3. **Skill and Experience Requirements**:
   - **High IQ Requirement**: Learning to trade successfully is not accessible to everyone, especially those lacking a high IQ (T44).
   - **Logical Mindset**: Trading requires a logical mindset and understanding of numbers, projections, risk management, and money management (T40).

4. **Complexity and Risk Management**:
   - **Foundation Building**: Trading requires a solid foundation, akin to building with concrete at the base and not just playing cards (T45).
   - **Continuous Learning and Adaptation**: Effective trading involves continuous learning and adapting strategies based on real market conditions, not just theoretical knowledge (T46).

5. **Emotional and Psychological Factors**:
   - **Self-Review Importance**: Emotional and psychological factors such as fear, desperation, and confidence levels significantly impact trading decisions (T51).
   - **Journaling and Self-Assessment**: Keeping a trading journal helps in assessing emotional states, decision-making processes, and identifying areas for improvement (T52).

6. **Market Volatility and Uncertainty**:
   - **Unpredictability**: Markets are inherently unpredictable, and no one can accurately predict future market movements (T53).
   - **Skill and Strategy Over Luck**: Success in trading depends more on skill and strategy than luck, emphasizing the need for a disciplined approach (T53).

In conclusion, while external trading strategies can provide valuable insights and initial frameworks, they should not be relied upon exclusively. Adapting these strategies to personal circumstances, maintaining a critical mindset, and continuously refining one's approach are essential for sustainable success in trading.
