2. **Concise and Specific Instructions**:
   - **Markdown for Coding**: Anthropic’s system prompts emphasize the use of Markdown for coding, ensuring that code segments returned by the AI models are formatted correctly and are easily readable.
   - **Avoiding Redundant Information**: Prompts are designed to avoid unnecessary details and ensure that responses are concise and directly relevant to the user's query. For instance, Claude’s prompts do not mention information about itself unless directly pertinent to the query.

3. **Handling Controversial Topics**:
   - **Ethical Handling**: Anthropic’s system prompts instruct the AI models to handle controversial topics ethically and objectively, avoiding biases and providing transparent information. This ensures that the models do not engage in stereotyping or provide false information.

4. **Versatile Responses**:
   - **Complex Queries**: Anthropic’s system prompts are designed to provide thorough responses to complex and open-ended questions, while offering concise answers to simpler queries.
   - **Logical Reasoning**: The prompts include instructions for systematic thinking and problem-solving, especially for tasks like math problems and logic puzzles. For example, Claude processes these tasks step-by-step before providing a final answer.
