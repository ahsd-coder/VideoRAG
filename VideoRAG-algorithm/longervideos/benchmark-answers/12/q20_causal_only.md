### Process of Making 'Nang', Grilled Bread in Xinjiang

#### Ingredients
- **Dough**: Typically made from wheat flour, water, yeast, and sometimes milk or eggs to enhance flavor and texture.
- **Seasonings**: Often includes salt, sugar, and sometimes spices like cumin or sesame seeds.

#### Tools
- **Wooden Surface**: Used for kneading and shaping the dough.
- **Cooking Vessel**: A traditional clay oven or a large, circular metal pan heated over an open flame.
- **Tongs**: Essential for safely handling the hot bread during cooking and removal.
- **Oven Stones**: Sometimes used for even heat distribution and baking.

#### Cooking Techniques
1. **Preparation**:
   - Start by mixing the flour, yeast, and seasonings to form a dough.
   - Knead the dough on a wooden surface until smooth and elastic.
   - Allow the dough to rise until doubled in size.
   
2. **Shaping**:
   - Divide the dough into manageable portions.
   - Roll each portion into a ball and flatten it into a disk shape.
   - Optionally, sprinkle sesame seeds or other toppings on the flattened dough.

3. **Cooking**:
   - Preheat the cooking vessel (clay oven or metal pan) to ensure it is very hot.
   - Place the flattened dough into the hot cooking vessel, using tongs for safety.
   - Cook the dough until it puffs up and develops a golden-brown crust.
   - Flip the bread halfway through to ensure even cooking on both sides.
   
4. **Handling**:
   - Use tongs to remove the bread from the cooking vessel once fully cooked.
   - Cool slightly before serving to allow the crust to crisp up further.
