   - **Addressing Pandemic Concerns**: The ongoing pandemic and health crises like monkeypox and polio have added layers of complexity to their work, requiring journalists to handle sensitive topics with care and accuracy.

3. **Ensuring Accuracy and Credibility**:
   - **Fact-Checking and Verification**: In an era of misinformation and fake news, journalists must meticulously verify information to maintain their credibility.    - **Use of Technology in Reporting**: Utilizing advanced tools such as drones and satellite imagery can aid in accurate reporting, especially in covering natural disasters and emergencies. However, the misuse of technology can lead to ethical dilemmas and privacy concerns.
